Cosmetic Dermatology

“Beauty is only skin deep.”Can we deny that? No!

Skin defines a person's appearance. Any change in skin appearance, such as scarring, can affect a person both mentally and physically. We at Dadu Medical Centre in Delhi, Vasant Vihar (South Delhi), Rajouri Garden (West Delhi), provide the diagnosis, treatment and prevention of skin conditions that adversely influence our appearance. Most of these skin changes are easily diagnosed by visible inspection of the skin. There are various treatments available to remove or lessen the effects of these skin problems.

Laser, Intense Pulsed Light (IPL), chemical peels, Anti Wrinkle Injections, fillers, medications and cosmeceuticals are some of the treatments available in cosmetic dermatology. These treatments need to be carefully tailored according to each patient’s needs. We are committed to offering the highest quality of cosmetic dermatology treatments in Delhi. Our cosmetic dermatology procedures aim to reduce blemishes, improve skin tone, and give you a new radiant look.

Cosmetic Dermatology in Delhi

Advanced Skin and Hair Treatments at Dadu Medical Centre

Looking good and feeling confident in your own skin is something that matters deeply to most people and cosmetic dermatology medical skin treatment in Delhi at Dadu Medical Centre exists precisely to help people achieve that, not through shortcuts or temporary fixes, but through clinically sound, medically supervised treatments that address the actual causes of the concerns rather than just covering them up.

Cosmetic dermatology is the branch of skin medicine that focuses on improving the appearance of the skin, hair, and nails using treatments that are both safe and evidence-based. It covers a very wide range from reducing acne scars and reversing the signs of ageing, to removing unwanted hair permanently, lightening persistent pigmentation, managing stretch marks, and enhancing facial features through non-surgical procedures.

At Dadu Medical Centre, one of the most established cosmetic dermatology clinics in Delhi, Dr. Nivedita Dadu has built a practice that combines deep clinical knowledge with access to the most advanced aesthetic technologies available. The clinic treats every patient as a unique individual; no two people have the same skin, the same concerns, or the same expectations, and the treatment plans are built around this reality rather than a standard off-the-shelf approach. The clinic operates from Vasant Vihar in South Delhi and Rajouri Garden in West Delhi, serving patients from across Delhi and the NCR region, and every cosmetic procedure is performed under full medical supervision with treatments tailored to the patient's skin type, concern, and goals.

Treatments Available

Treatments available at the clinic

The range of cosmetic dermatology treatments available at Dadu Medical Centre is one of the most comprehensive at any skin clinic in Delhi.

For people dealing with the visible signs of ageing fine lines, skin laxity, sagging jawline, loss of facial volume, and wrinkles the clinic offers anti-wrinkle injections using botulinum toxin, hyaluronic acid dermal fillers for volume restoration and lip enhancement, HIFU (High Intensity Focused Ultrasound) for non-surgical face lifting and skin tightening at the SMAS level, thread lift for structural facial lifting, Tixel thermomechanical skin resurfacing, ResurFX fractional erbium laser, RF skin tightening, collagen induction therapy, Vampire Facial with PRP, and several exclusive treatments developed at the clinic including GFR (Growth Factor Rejuvenation), DMC-DART (DNA Repair Technique), and DMC-Insta Regen.

For acne both active breakouts and the scars they leave behind the clinic offers a full spectrum of treatments including chemical peels, medifacials, Acne Zap Laser, MNRF (Microneedling Radiofrequency), Fractional CO2 Laser, Erbium Fibre Laser, and dermaroller sessions.

For pigmentation, melasma, dark spots, and uneven skin tone, treatments include laser toning, chemical peels, DivaGenesis, Crystal Glaze, photofacial using IPL, and medicated skincare programmes.

For hair fall, the clinic offers DMC-HGP (Hair Growth Promoters), Advanced HGP, Advanced HGP 2.0, Root Restore Therapy, and scalp mesotherapy.

Laser hair removal using the Soprano ICE system is available for the face and full body with virtually no pain and no downtime.

For stretch marks and body skin concerns, treatments include MNRF, Erbium Fibre Laser, Tixel, and Dermaroller.

Exclusive proprietary treatments including the Magic Blend, Crystal Glaze, Martini, Miracle Infusum, Bloody Mary Peel, and DMC-Bling Shots round out the clinic's unique cosmetic offerings that are available only at Dadu Medical Centre.

Expert Answers

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is cosmetic dermatology and how is it different from a regular facial at a salon?

Cosmetic dermatology uses medically proven treatments performed by qualified dermatologists including lasers, injectables, peels, and devices to address actual skin concerns, unlike salon treatments that only work on the surface.

What cosmetic skin treatments are available at Dadu Medical Centre in Delhi?

The clinic offers anti-wrinkle injections, fillers, HIFU, laser hair removal, acne scar treatments, pigmentation treatments, hair fall therapies, medifacials, thread lift, chemical peels, and many exclusive proprietary treatments.

Is cosmetic dermatology safe for Indian skin tones?

Yes, all treatments at the clinic are selected and calibrated specifically for Indian skin types. Dr. Nivedita Dadu has extensive experience treating patients across all skin tones safely.

Do I need a consultation before starting any cosmetic treatment?

Yes, every cosmetic treatment at Dadu Medical Centre begins with a proper skin assessment and consultation where the right treatment plan is built around the individual patient's specific concerns.

Can cosmetic treatments help with both acne scars and skin ageing at the same time?

Yes, some treatments like MNRF, Erbium Fibre Laser, and Fractional CO2 address both scars and ageing-related concerns together. The doctor recommends the best approach after examining the skin.

How many sessions of cosmetic treatment are typically needed?

The number of sessions varies by treatment and concern. Most laser and injectable treatments need three to eight sessions, while some procedures like fillers need only one per visit.

Are the results from cosmetic dermatology permanent?

Some treatments like laser hair removal provide long-term permanent reduction. Others like anti-wrinkle injections and fillers need periodic maintenance. Results from laser scar and pigmentation treatments are long-lasting with proper aftercare.

Can both men and women get cosmetic dermatology treatments?

Yes, all treatments at Dadu Medical Centre are available for both men and women. Protocols are adjusted based on individual skin type, concern, and desired outcome.

Which clinic in Delhi should I visit for cosmetic dermatology?

Dadu Medical Centre in Vasant Vihar and Rajouri Garden, led by Dr. Nivedita Dadu, is a trusted and comprehensive cosmetic dermatology clinic in Delhi offering the widest range of medical-grade aesthetic treatments.
