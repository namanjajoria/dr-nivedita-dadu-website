Tixel is a new non-laser fractional system of skin resurfacing that is based on thermomechanical ablation technology. It helps in Acne Scars Removal Treatment. How is Tixel different from Lasers?

LASERS are a technology based on light. The light from the lasers interacts with the skin and causes effects by heating the skin. TIXEL is based on heat. Tixel transfers heat directly to the skin itself with its patented titanium tips.

Why is Tixel better than Traditional CO2 Lasers?

  • Tixel is a is a non-invasive and comfortable treatment for rejuvenating the skin uses pure heat energy and gives results that are similar to CO2 lasers in return but takes significantly less downtime.
  • Tixel and traditional CO2 lasers work similarly, both deliver pure heat energy to the skin and similar results but TIXEL does not cause the same amount of damage to the internal layers of the skin as lasers.
  • Tixel micropores are free from necrotic tissue unlike the typical micropores present in fractional C02 lasers.

How does TIXEL work?

TIXEL uses a Titanium tip with tiny pins made of biocompatible material, having a pyramid like a shape with small contact points which are about 20 microns in size and cover a treatment area of 1cm?2;. The pins are heated to a temperature of 400°C, as similar to the temperature usually generated by other fractional CO? laser beams. The thermal energy stored in the pins is quickly transferred to the skin that will last only a few milliseconds, through a prefixed contact time and contact depth. It can be used at different depths and with different contact times that helps TIXEL to deliver different and effective results.

Using medium penetration and a high contact time that allows TIXEL to work like an ablative laser. It is an ideal solution for giving good improvements in acne scars, stretch marks or fine lines of the skin. It also allows TIXEL for tightening the skin and to treat open pores and fine lines. Using very short contact and very superficial penetration that will help to make open channels in the skin through which actives like (anti aging creams, anti pigmentation creams, Collagen Induction Therapy) and medicines can be easily delivered.

The thermal energy is transferred safely and quickly as compared to the traditional CO2 lasers and the subsequent evaporation of water from the skin occurs resulting in the formation of tiny micropores in the treated sites. This evaporates the upper layers in a controlled manner so that there will be no chance of burns in the tissue. Using pure heat energy, the tip of the Tixel machine partially touches the skin which causes neat, clean micro-injuries.

Acne Scar Removal Treatment in Delhi – Get Clearer Skin at Dadu Medical Centre

Acne scars are one of the most common skin concerns that people deal with long after the acne itself is gone, and finding the right acne scar removal treatment in Delhi can make a real difference in how your skin looks and feels. Some people get just a few light marks that fade on their own within months, but for many others, especially those who had severe or deep acne, the scars go much deeper into the skin layers and do not go away without proper medical treatment. The type of scar matters a lot when it comes to choosing the right treatment. There are different kinds of acne scars ice pick scars, which are narrow and deep like small holes; boxcar scars, which are wider with sharp edges; rolling scars, which give the skin an uneven or wavy look; and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, which shows up as flat dark or reddish marks after a pimple heals. Each of these responds differently to different treatments, which is why a one-size-fits-all approach does not work well for acne scar removal. At Dadu Medical Centre, one of the trusted acne scar treatment clinics in Delhi, Dr. Nivedita Dadu assesses each patient's skin individually before recommending a treatment plan. The clinic is located in Vasant Vihar, South Delhi, and Rajouri Garden, West Delhi, and offers a range of clinically proven options to address all types of acne scarring effectively.

Understanding why acne scars form in the first place helps in deciding the best course of action. When a pimple becomes deeply inflamed, it damages the skin tissue underneath. As the skin tries to heal itself, it produces collagen to repair the damage. If too little collagen is produced, the result is a depressed scar that sits below the skin surface. If too much collagen forms, the result is a raised scar. The flat dark marks that many people confuse with scars are not true scars at all they are pigmentation changes that happen as part of the skin's healing response. While pigmentation marks often respond well to chemical peels and laser toning, the deeper depressed or raised scars need treatments that work on rebuilding the skin's collagen structure from within. This is where procedures like Fractional CO2 Laser, Microneedling Radiofrequency (MNRF), Erbium Fibre Laser, Dermaroller, and Tixel come in. These are all available at the acne scar removal clinic in Delhi at Dadu Medical Centre, and the choice of treatment is made after a thorough skin evaluation.

Fractional CO2 Laser is one of the most effective acne scar treatments in Delhi for moderate to deep scars. It works by creating tiny controlled injuries in the skin, which triggers the body's natural healing process and pushes the skin to produce fresh collagen. Over the next few weeks, the skin gradually firms up and the scars become shallower and less visible. The procedure has a short recovery period of about three to five days and gives very good results after a few sessions. MNRF, or Micro Needling Radiofrequency, is another popular option that combines fine needles with radiofrequency energy. The needles create micro-channels in the skin while the radiofrequency heat goes deeper to stimulate collagen production. This treatment works very well for rolling and boxcar scars and is safe for Indian skin tones. Dermaroller is a simpler form of needling that works on surface-level scars and also helps in improving overall skin texture. Erbium Fibre Laser and Tixel are newer technologies that resurface the skin with minimal downtime and are suitable for people with lighter scarring or those who cannot afford much recovery time. All these treatments are performed by experienced acne scar doctors in Delhi at Dadu Medical Centre under proper medical supervision.

One of the most common mistakes people make is trying to treat acne scars on their own using over-the-counter creams or home remedies. While some mild marks may respond to topical products over time, true depressed or raised scars almost never improve with just creams. In fact, some products can irritate the skin further and cause more pigmentation. Getting a proper consultation from a qualified skin specialist is the most important first step. Dr. Nivedita Dadu and her team guide each patient through the available options, set realistic expectations, and create a treatment plan that fits the patient's skin condition, downtime availability, and budget. The number of sessions needed depends on the severity of the scars mild scarring may improve with three to four sessions while deeper scarring may need six or more. Sessions are usually spaced four to six weeks apart to give the skin time to heal and regenerate between treatments.

Aftercare is a very important part of getting good results from any acne scar treatment in Delhi. Patients are advised to use a broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher every day without fail, since sun exposure can darken the treated areas and slow down healing. Avoid scrubbing or picking at the skin during the recovery phase. Stay well hydrated and follow the post-care routine given by the doctor. Avoid using retinoids or active ingredients on the treated skin unless specifically recommended. With the right treatment plan and proper aftercare, most patients see a significant improvement in their acne scars over the course of their sessions.
