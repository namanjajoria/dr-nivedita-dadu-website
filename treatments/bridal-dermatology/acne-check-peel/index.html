Acne Check Peel Treatment

4.8(1,067 Reviews)30–45 mins

Oily skin, a few pimples, blackheads across the nose. Most people with acne-prone skin have dealt with all three at the same time, and usually tried a scrub that made things worse. If you're searching for an acne peel treatment in Delhi, there's a good chance you're done experimenting.

The Acne Check Peel is a chemical peel with a black vinegar base. Black vinegar has antibacterial and exfoliating properties, so the peel can clear out pores and work on oil without anyone scrubbing your face. Before it goes on, the blackheads are extracted, which means your pores look cleaner by the time you leave. Most people are back at work the same day.

How the Acne Check Peel Works

A clogged pore is basically dead skin and oil stuck together, with bacteria feeding on it. The peel loosens that dead skin so the pore can clear, and its antibacterial action goes after the bacteria behind breakouts. Repeat it over a few sessions and the skin also tends to produce less oil.

There's a side benefit for marks. Faster skin turnover means old brown spots from pimples slowly lighten, and the surface starts to feel smoother. The doctor also controls how deep it goes, adding a second coat only if the first one hasn't done enough, so it's a gentler option for skin that flares up with stronger treatments.

What It Helps With

Benefits

The big plus is that it deals with several problems in one sitting. Pores get cleared without scrubbing, blackheads are taken out on the spot, and the antibacterial effect helps settle breakouts. Keep going for a few sessions and oil usually calms down while dark marks fade.

And it's easy to live with. There's normally no visible peeling, and most people go straight back to their day.

Ideal Candidates

If your skin gets greasy by midday and you're always dealing with blackheads, congestion or pimples, this peel is worth considering. Same if breakouts leave brown marks behind. It also works for Indian skin that picks up pigmentation easily, or that tends to react to harsher treatments.

Process & Techniques

Plan for 30 to 45 minutes. Your face is cleaned first to get rid of makeup and oil, and then the blackheads and whiteheads are extracted. After that, one coat of the peel goes on and stays for 3 minutes. It stings a bit at first, but that fades within a few minutes.

The doctor then checks for frosting, a faint white film that shows how deep the peel has gone. No frosting means a second coat may be applied for another 3 minutes. The peel is wiped off with a wet tissue, and a soothing product and sunscreen finish things off.

How many sessions? Most people need 4 to 6, with a gap of 2 to 3 weeks between each. To keep skin in good shape afterwards, a session every 4 to 6 weeks is usually enough.

After the peel: There's very little downtime. For the next 4 or 5 days your skin might look a touch red or slightly darker, and that settles without any treatment. Visible peeling isn't common.

Aftercare: Stick to a gentle, non-comedogenic moisturiser and a broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or more every day. Hold off on scrubs and strong actives unless your dermatologist tells you otherwise. And try not to pick at pimples or blackheads at home, however tempting.

FAQs

What exactly is the Acne Check Peel?

It's a black vinegar-based chemical peel. It unclogs pores and helps cut down oil and acne bacteria, and you don't need time off afterwards.

Will it sting?

Slightly, for the first few minutes. Most people find it easy to sit through.

How many sessions do I need?

Usually four to six, two to three weeks apart. Occasional maintenance sessions help after that.

Will my skin peel?

Not visibly, in most cases. It may look a little red or darker for four or five days before going back to normal.

Does it get rid of blackheads?

It helps a lot. They're extracted before the peel, and the peel keeps pores clearer afterwards.

My skin is very oily. Is this right for me?

Yes. The peel was made for oily, acne-prone skin.

Can it lighten old acne marks?

Over time, yes. Most marks fade gradually across four to six sessions.

What does it cost?

About ₹1,500 to ₹3,500 a session. Your doctor will confirm the exact figure at the consultation.
