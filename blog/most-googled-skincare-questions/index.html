Most googled skincare questions

Good skin has tremendous social and mental benefits. Good skin boosts self-confidence and provides extra bounce in the step of looking good. A proper skincare routine can help in preventing skin conditions like premature aging, wrinkles, acne, oiliness, and so on. Most skin problems are intense and not seeking treatment often causes permanent damage to the skin. Skincare routine does not have to be complicated. Dedicating just a few minutes daily can help in achieving flawless skin. But, the problem is there are so many myths and doubts about skin problems that most people end up following the wrong advice.

Here's an attempt to help y'all with the right answers to the most googled skincare questions:

How to get rid of oily skin?

People with oily skin , face many skin problems like clogged pores, open pores, comedones, acne, acne scars, etc. Dealing with oily skin is a daily struggle. Oily skin is caused by excess production of sebum. Too much production of sebum makes the skin appear shiny and prone to spotty flare-ups. A common mistake that people with oily skin make is not using a moisturizer. They must use a water-based or gel-based moisturizer and incorporate the use of blotting papers to lessen the shine throughout the day. The basic key to get rid of oily skin is to keep skin clean and stay hydrated. One must use products that contain zinc gluconate to help reduce oil secretion.

What is the most important part of a skincare routine?

Sun damage increases the risk of skin cancer and it also damages the skin and causes problems like premature aging and dark spots. So, it is highly recommended for one and all to apply sunscreen daily. A broad-spectrum sunscreen with a minimum SPF of 30 and PA rating +++ is recommended . This is an essential morning ritual that must be followed to protect the skin. Sunscreen must be reapplied throughout the day to keep the skin protected. Washing the face with a gentle cleanser and applying a moisturizer are also integral parts of an ideal skincare routine.

How can I prevent premature aging?

The signs of aging are clearly visible on the face in the form of wrinkles, fine lines, dark spots. With age, skin loses elasticity and collagen. Sun exposure can be the main reason for premature aging. So, never skip applying broad-spectrum sunscreen. Besides this, one can use under eye creams and topical creams that contain retinoids and antioxidants. But, do not self-medicate. One must always consult a dermatologist before using any medications or creams on the skin.

How to take care of sensitive skin?

Sensitive skin needs proper care and caution while choosing skincare products. Always wash the sensitive skin gently and avoid the application of harsh cleansers. One must avoid using products that contain irritating substances, rather one must look for products that are marketed as sensitive skin products. Sometimes, there may be certain ingredients that aggravate skin sensitivity. So, it is crucial to identify and avoid them.

How to get rid of uneven skin tone?

Uneven skin tone is caused by an uneven distribution of melanin pigment. Uneven skin tone can also be caused by a buildup of dead skin cells. In such a case, one must exfoliate their skin several times a week with a gentle exfoliator.

Pigmentation and uneven skin tone can also happen because of excess sun exposure and hormonal imbalance. This can be corrected by using serums, creams, or moisturizers which contain Vitamin C. These help in correcting uneven skin tone.

How to find out individual skin type?

Skin is classified in a few categories which include normal, oily, dry, combination, and sensitive types. It is imperative to identify the individual skin type to be able to choose the right products and take proper care of skin health. An easy way to determine the skin type is to wash it gently at night and leave the skin free of any product. In the morning, you'll wake up with dry, oily, or oily skin only in the T zone (combination skin). People with sensitive skin are mostly aware of their skin type because even the simplest of products like a moisturizer may irritate their skin. Whether normal, dry, oily, combination, or sensitive skin; everyone needs to take care of it.

Skincare problems must not be neglected and one must pay full attention to how the skin reacts to various products. Everyone desires to have a healthy, rejuvenated, and vibrant skin but it requires constant care and effort. One must not take skin concerns lightly as these can damage the skin permanently. The application of skin products without consulting a dermatologist can also harm the skin.

Be wise, choose well!
