How DMC-HGP® Works

In a lot of hair loss, follicles don't die straight away. They shrink. Each new hair comes out a little finer and shorter than the last, until some follicles stop producing visible hair at all. Doctors call this miniaturisation.

Growth factors are proteins your body uses for repair. When they're concentrated from your blood and placed right at the follicles, they can help reverse that shrinking and nudge resting follicles back into growth. Shampoos, serums and oils mostly work on the scalp's surface. HGP goes to the root, which is where the problem actually sits.

Sometimes HGP is paired with other treatments. If your blood tests show a deficiency, oral medicines may be prescribed alongside. It can also be combined with mesotherapy, and Advanced HGP adds microneedling and low-level laser therapy in the same session for better penetration and circulation.

What It Can Help With

HGP is used for male and female pattern hair loss, general thinning all over the scalp, and hair fall after an illness or a stressful period. It's also used for a thinning or receding hairline, a widening parting and a thin crown. It's often suggested for people who've tried minoxidil or other topical treatments and haven't seen enough change.

Benefits

Because it uses material from your own body, there's no foreign substance going into your scalp, and the risk of an allergic reaction is very low. It works at the follicle rather than the surface, and it can be targeted to exactly where thinning is worst, whether that's the hairline, temples or crown. Sessions take under an hour, downtime is minimal, and HGP slots easily into a wider plan with mesotherapy, medicines or Advanced HGP if your hair needs more support.

Ideal Candidates

Men and women with early to moderate thinning tend to respond best, especially when the follicles are weakened rather than completely gone. It also suits people dealing with stress- or illness-related shedding, or anyone whose results from topical treatments have plateaued. Before starting, the doctor examines your scalp and may order blood tests to check for hormonal, thyroid or nutritional causes, so those can be treated too.

Process & Techniques

Plan for around 45 to 60 minutes.

First, your scalp is assessed and blood tests may be done to look for underlying causes. On the day, a small amount of blood is drawn from your arm, similar to a routine test, and spun in a centrifuge to concentrate the growth factors. Your scalp is numbed with a local anaesthetic, and sometimes the anaesthetic is mixed into the HGP solution itself. In some cases, ultrasound imaging is used to find the areas most likely to respond.

The solution is then injected systematically across the thinning areas, such as the hairline, crown and temples. With the numbing in place, most people feel very little. You'll get aftercare instructions before you leave.

How many sessions? Most people need several sessions spaced a few weeks apart, followed by regular maintenance. Your doctor will plan the exact schedule after seeing your scalp.

What results look like: Many people notice less shedding within the first few sessions. Thicker, denser hair usually becomes visible after about four to six sessions, and the hairline improves gradually over the same period. Results vary from person to person.

Recovery and risks: Downtime is minimal and most people are back to normal the next day. As with any injection, there's a small risk of scar tissue, calcification or injury to a blood vessel.

Aftercare:

  • Don't wash your scalp for about 24 hours
  • Keep your scalp out of direct sun for a few days
  • Skip swimming and heavy workouts for a few days
  • Eat a balanced diet with enough protein and fluids
