Hydrafacial or Silk Peel? Which one is the best for your skin?

Confused between these two famous medifacials?

Well, both of them have their own benefits. With a thorough understanding of the treatments, one can make a better decision. So without any further ado, let's start.

First, let’s understand what is Hydrafacial?

Hydrafacial is a non-invasive lunchtime facial that involves a combination of serums, chemical exfoliation, hydration, and extraction at the same time to treat your skin. It is a painless and uncomplicated procedure. The best part about it is that it is suitable for all skin types and age groups with its primary objective of rejuvenating and hydrating the skin.

Patients have experienced a noticeable difference with hydrafacial in Delhi at Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Dermatology Clinic. As this medifacial also stimulates collagen and elastin the results are wonderful.

This lunchtime facial doesn’t have any downtime, recovery, or major post-care. It requires minimal post-care including photoprotection i.e sunscreen which as a matter of fact is required after every treatment. Also, it is a quick lunchtime procedure. It is totally appropriate to be done right before parties.

How do I know Hydrafacial is the right choice for me?

Hydrafacial is a 45-60 minute treatment that initially helps to remove dead skin cells, dirt, oil, and other debris from the surface of your skin. The steps include a combination of glycolic and salicylic acids that effectively exfoliate the outer layer of skin. This micro-exfoliation helps serums permeate the skin easily during treatment.

Hydrafacial is formulated to treat every part of your skin. It temporarily minimizes common concerns like dry or dull skin and comedones with just one treatment. The results may last for up to a week or so. For long-lasting results, successive sessions are recommended.

Now, let’s talk about Silk Peel Dermalinfusion

Silk Peel in Delhi at Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Dermatology Clinic is a very popular medifacial. It is a top-notch mechanical exfoliating treatment designed to give the skin a noticeable boost in hydration and natural glow. This therapeutic treatment consists of a handheld diamond tip device to exfoliate dry and dead skin cells from the surface of the skin, accompanied by an infusion of hydrating serums, brightening serums, and various pore unclogging serums.

The treatment helps the skin breathe and look healthy and supple. It can be customized according to the patient’s skin type and skin concerns like- acne, pigmentation, open pores, and dehydration.

Is it the right treatment for you?

This treatment works well for all skin types. It is painless and gentle for patients with sensitive skin also. This treatment will improve conditions such as acne, clogged or large pores, oily skin, dark spots, and many other common skin concerns.

It is again a quick treatment of 30-45 minutes which gives glow and long-lasting healthy skin.

Yes, it's difficult to distinguish between the two as both the treatments provide satisfying results. But, if you look at them carefully, both serve different benefits. Well, one thing is for sure that none of them would disappoint you!
