How to Include Hyaluronic Acid in Daily Skincare?

Hyaluronic acid has always been one of the popular and most desirable ingredients that we look out for while choosing our skincare products. The sole reason for this is its ability to attract an incredible amount of water content into the skin.

Multiple factors like environmental stress, pollution, dryness, temperature, ageing can lead to parched and flaky skin. Hyaluronic acid is one of the most loved ingredients to get back the glow and lustre of the skin.

How Exactly Hyaluronic Acid Benefits our Skin?

The youthful and plump look of our skin is due to the presence of hyaluronic acid that is produced naturally in our body. With ageing the production slows down gradually and hence, it makes the skin dry, flaky, parched leading to the appearance of fine lines, wrinkles and open pores. Hyaluronic acid does not only help in hydrating the skin by providing a surge of moisture but also keeps the skin barrier intact by making it soft and plump. It attracts moisture and binds it to the skin surface which helps skin cells stay intensely moisturised. Hyaluronic acid heals the dryness of skin and treats rough patches by providing built-in protection against further damage whilst keeping skin supple and plump.

Which Skincare Products have Hyaluronic Acid in Them?

Hyaluronic acid is such a potent humectant that it has become a very common skincare ingredient. It is found in most skincare products. It is generally advised for all skin types and is found in various skincare products. It is most commonly found in anti-ageing creams that as hyaluronic acid works well with skin favourites like vitamin C or retinol. It increases the potency and results of these ingredients. Even if ageing is not the present skin concern, hyaluronic acid can still be incorporated in the skincare regimen due to its moisturising and hydrating abilities.

Hyaluronic acid is a part of nourishing under eye creams and concentrated skin serums that are used for skin pigmentation, dullness, and glow. They work well in conjugation with vitamins and nutrients. For those who stay in dry and cold countries, their skin starves for moisturisation and ages quickly. To prevent this, it is advised to use a high concentration of hyaluronic acid to ensure locking in of moisture all around the day.

How to Incorporate Hyaluronic Acid in Daily Routine?

Hyaluronic acid can be found in hydrating cleansers which help remove dirt and excess of makeup from dry skin whilst not stripping off its natural oils. Further, day and night serums can be chosen which have HA as one of its ingredients. In case someone has super dry skin they can opt for a high concentration of HA in their moisturiser. If someone is facing issues of dry under-eye, fine lines and wrinkles start appearing in the area. In such a case, HA containing under eye creams can be opted for to see effective improvements.

HA is a very gentle ingredient and serves well for all types of skin. This ingredient works to lock the moisture of the skin to keep the skin barrier intact along with hydrating and adding a surge of hydration to the skin by absorbing moisture. Consult a dermatologist to ensure to get the right concentration and apt products as per individual skin needs.
