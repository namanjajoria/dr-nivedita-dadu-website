1. What is a DMC-Fraxogenus treatment?

The DMC-FRAXOGENUS is a carbon dioxide laser that accurately removes deep outer layers of damaged skin and stimulates the regeneration of healthy and new skin underneath. The DMC-FRAXOGENUS treats fine to moderately deep wrinkles, photodamage, scarring, skin tone, texture, elasticity; hence, considered one of the best anti ageing treatments in Delhi.

2. How much time does a DMC-Fraxogenus treatment require?

The time depends on the area that is under treatment; However, usually, it takes two hours or less to complete - says the best anti ageing doctor in Delhi. This may include an additional 30 minutes for topical numbing to be applied before the treatment.

3. Does a DMC-Fraxogenus treatment hurt?

The DMC-FRAXOGENUS is a minimally invasive laser resurfacing treatment in Delhi. It might cause slight discomfort at the start, but we ensure to our patients they will be comfortable throughout the whole procedure. The sensation that is often felt is similar to a “pinprick” sensation.

4. When will the results after a DMC-Fraxogenus be visible?

After the skin heals, it may take up to 3 weeks - says the best cosmetologist in Delhi. In these 3 weeks, patients may experience a part of their skin appearing moderately better in terms of skin texture and tone. Full results may be visible in 3-6 months with multiple sessions.

5. Till How long do results from the DMC-Fraxogenus last?

Improvements from the DMC-Fraxogenus anti ageing treatment in Delhi can be noticeable for many years after the treatment. Results can be prolonged with diligent use of SPF+, avoided sun exposure, and the right at-home skincare maintenance will maximize results.

6. Which areas does DMC-Fraxogenus treatment treat?

The DMC-FRAXOGENUS is done on multiple body parts. However, the most popular areas to treat are the full face and neck. So, if you are looking for a Face Rejuvenation treatment in Delhi, the DMC-FRAXOGENUS will be one of the best options.

7. What is the downtime associated with a DMC-Fraxogenus treatment?

The downtime associated with a DMC-Fraxogenus treatment is 7-10 days for healing before you can go out in public. Your skin will scab and peel 2-7 days after the treatment and will be pinkish for the next 3-4 weeks. The exact healing time varies from person to person as per our skin specialists in Delhi.

8. How many DMC-FRAXOGENUS treatment sessions will I need?

According to us, most patients only need one DMC-FRAXOGENUS treatment to see remarkable results. However, some patients with deeper wrinkles and fine lines or scarring will need multiple treatment sessions to see results.

9. Which candidates should restrain from a DMC-Fraxogenus treatment?

The DMC-Fraxogenus treatment may not be recommended for those with certain health problems or those taking certain medications. Those with a past record of difficulty in healing or scarring are not appropriate candidates, as well as those with bleeding disorders. Those who are pregnant or breastfeeding are also not a candidate for the DMC-Fraxogenus skin rejuvenation treatment in Delhi.
