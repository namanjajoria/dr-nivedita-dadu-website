What Makes DivaGenesis Different

Most glow facials stay on the surface. They clean and hydrate, and the effect fades in a few days. DivaGenesis goes further because it combines two of our advanced techniques, laser and micro-channeling, after the skin has been properly prepped.

The laser works on pigment that sits deeper in the skin, which is why dark spots and uneven patches actually start to lighten over time. The micro-channeling step then delivers a serum cocktail into the skin instead of letting it sit on top. Each session is customised at Dadu Medical Centre based on your skin type, your concern and how your skin has responded before.

Process & Techniques

A DivaGenesis session takes about 45 to 60 minutes and runs in three steps.

  • Deep exfoliation: A diamond crystal tip gently buffs away dead cells, dullness and surface tan. This clears the way so the next two steps can work properly.
  • Laser: The laser reaches the deeper layers of skin and breaks down the pigment causing dark spots. It also works on photo-damage, uneven tone, fine lines and laxity.
  • Micro-channeling with serum infusion: Fine micro-needles, paired with gentle suction, create tiny channels and lift the skin. A serum cocktail is infused through these channels for better absorption, and the process also nudges the skin's own healing response, supporting collagen and fibroblast activity.

How often? It depends on why you're coming in:

  • Before a party or as a first trial: a single session
  • Pigmentation correction: 4 to 6 sessions, once every 3 weeks
  • Anti-ageing and skin renewal: once every 3 to 4 weeks
  • Bridal prep: once a month for 3 months before the wedding
  • Maintenance: once a month

Aftercare: There's no downtime, so you can get on with your day. Use sunscreen daily and stick to the home routine your dermatologist suggests, so the results last longer.
