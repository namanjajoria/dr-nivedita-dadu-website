How Laser Hair Removal Works

The laser looks for the pigment inside the hair follicle. When it finds it, the light turns into heat and damages the follicle enough that it struggles to produce hair again.

There's a reason it takes several sessions. Hair grows in cycles, and the laser only works on follicles that are in their active growth phase at the time. Others are resting and will come up later, so sessions are spaced out to catch each new round. With every visit, a few more follicles stop working and the hair gets noticeably thinner.

Lasers We Use

Not every laser suits every skin and hair type, which is why the clinic has more than one. Your specialist chooses after looking at your skin tone, hair colour and thickness, and the area being treated.

  • Soprano ICE: a diode laser with built-in cooling, used across all skin tones and for both coarse and fine hair
  • Diode laser (810nm): suited to medium to dark skin with coarse, dark hair
  • Alexandrite laser (755nm): better for fair to medium skin with fine to medium hair
  • Titanium Soprano: a multi-wavelength system that can be used on all skin and hair types

Older hair removal lasers carried a real risk of burns or pigment changes on darker skin. Today's diode and Soprano systems are considered safe for most Indian skin types, provided they're used by experienced professionals.

Process & Techniques

  • Consultation: Your skin tone, hair type and the areas you want treated are assessed, and the right laser is chosen.
  • Prep: Shave the area before your session. Avoid waxing or threading, because the laser needs the hair root in place to work.
  • Treatment: The laser is moved over the area in passes. With cooling systems like Soprano ICE, most people feel warmth rather than pain.
  • After the session: Treated hairs start shedding within one to two weeks.

How many sessions? Most areas need 6 to 8 sessions, spaced about four to six weeks apart. Beard shaping usually needs 4 to 6. The reduction becomes more visible with each sitting. Because hormones can wake up dormant follicles later on, a maintenance session once or twice a year may be needed.

Between sessions: Shaving is fine. Waxing and threading are not.

FAQs

How many sessions of laser hair removal will I need?

Usually 6 to 8, spaced four to six weeks apart. It varies with the area, hair density and skin type.

Is laser hair removal permanent?

It gives long-lasting reduction rather than a guarantee that no hair will ever return. Some people need a maintenance session once or twice a year.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe it as warmth, not pain, especially with cooling systems like Soprano ICE.

Can women remove facial hair with laser?

Yes. Upper lip, chin and full face treatments are common and considered safe when done correctly.

Does it work on dark Indian skin?

Yes. Diode and Soprano systems are designed with medium to dark skin in mind, and the specialist picks the setting for you.

When will I see results?

Treated hair starts shedding within one to two weeks. The reduction becomes clearer with each session.

Can I wax between sessions?

No. Only shave, because waxing and threading remove the root the laser needs to target.

What does laser hair removal cost in Delhi?

Per session, roughly ₹1,500 for small areas like the upper lip, going up to around ₹13,000 for larger areas like the back. The exact figure is confirmed after your consultation at Dadu Medical Centre.
