Cracked Heels That Won't Heal? Why DMC Solemate Might Be the Treatment You Need

If you've been dealing with cracked heels for a while now, chances are you've already tried the usual fixes: thick creams, wearing socks overnight, soaking feet in warm water, scrubbing away with a pumice stone. And chances are, none of it has genuinely stuck. If you're researching a proper Cracked Heels treatment in Delhi for heels that heal for a day and crack right back open, this is worth reading before trying yet another cream.

Why Standard Fixes Often Don't Last

This is one of the most common frustrations heard in clinic patients explaining they've tried everything, creams included, moisturized diligently, worn socks, soaked their feet, scrubbed with a pumice stone, and still, cracked heels return within a day or two of any visible improvement. If this cycle sounds familiar, the issue usually isn't a lack of effort. It's that the underlying cause needs a different kind of intervention altogether.

A Specific Cause Worth Knowing About: Keratoderma Climactericum

For women past a certain age, particularly those going through menopause, cracked heels can be linked to a specific condition called keratoderma climactericum. This happens due to hormonal changes during this life stage, and it's genuinely important to understand this connection, because it explains why standard pedicures and over-the-counter treatments consistently fail to resolve the problem.

If hormonal shifts are driving the cracking, no amount of foot scrubbing or standard moisturizing routine will get to the root of it. The skin's structure and moisture retention are being affected at a deeper level than surface treatment can reach.
