Contact Dermatitis

The rashes caused by Contact Dermatitis are red, irritable & itchy. There can be a large area of skin which gets affected by contact dermatitis. Most common factors that cause contact dermatitis are hair colours, artificial jewellary, or jeans buttons etc. Even after days, contact dermatitis fade only if the skin no longer comes in contact with the allergen or irritant.
