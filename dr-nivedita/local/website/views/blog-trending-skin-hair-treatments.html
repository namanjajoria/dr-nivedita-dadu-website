6 In-Clinic Skin & Hair Treatments: That Are Actually Worth the Hype

If you've been anywhere near a beauty forum or an Instagram reel lately, you've probably come across at least one of these treatments being talked about like it's the next big thing. Some of it is genuine hype backed by decent science, and some of it, honestly, is just marketing dressed up in fancy names. As someone who spends a good chunk of the day discussing these options with patients looking for reliable treatment in Delhi, I thought it was time to break down what's actually trending in 2025 and which of these are worth your time and money.

1. The Salmon Sperm Facial (Yes, That's Really What It's Called)

Let's start with the one that gets the most raised eyebrows purely because of its name. The "salmon sperm facial" isn't quite as literal as it sounds; what's actually being used is DNA extracted from salmon fish sperm, which is then processed into something called PDRN.

PDRN facials have picked up serious momentum because of what they actually do for the skin. They help with hydration, improve elasticity, calm down inflammation, and give that noticeable brightness and glow people are chasing. It's one of those treatments where the name grabs attention, but the results are what keep people coming back.

2. Liquid Lift The Injectable Face-Lift Alternative

This one's relatively new to India, though it's been used internationally for a while now. Liquid lift is an injectable treatment built around two key ingredients PLLA (poly-L-lactic acid) and CaHA (calcium hydroxyapatite).

What makes this one stand out is the combination of instant and long-term benefits. You get immediate lift and firmness right after the session, along with better hydration and elasticity, and the results genuinely stick around typically lasting up to two years or more. For anyone looking into anti-ageing options without going the surgical route, this has become one of the more talked-about choices at a good aesthetic treatment in Delhi.

3. NAD Injectables

NAD has been popular in IV drip form for a while, and now it's showing up as an injectable too even tablets are available these days. But injectable NAD is gaining traction specifically for its antioxidant properties.

These are typically given as skin boosters, often combined with glutathione, vitamin C, and hyaluronic acid alongside the NAD itself. The combination works on multiple fronts: antioxidant repair, brightening, and general anti-ageing support, all through a single injectable session.

Treatment Best For Key Ingredient(s)
Salmon Sperm Facial (PDRN) Hydration, elasticity, glow PDRN (Polydeoxyribonucleotide)
Liquid Lift Instant lift, long-term anti-ageing PLLA + CaHA
NAD Injectables Antioxidant repair, brightening NAD + Glutathione + Vitamin C + HA

4. GLP-1 Analogs for Weight Loss

This one probably needs no introduction at this point. GLP-1 analogs have taken over conversations around weight loss almost everywhere. These work by suppressing appetite, and they're given as a once-a-week injection, which makes compliance a lot easier compared to daily medication.

They're particularly relevant for people dealing with PCOD, hormonal imbalances, deranged sugar or cholesterol levels, or anyone simply struggling to lose weight despite trying the usual routes. It's also become a popular pre-wedding or festive season option for people wanting quicker, more consistent results.

5. Autologous Exosomes

Exosomes as a category aren't new plant-derived and animal-derived versions have been around for some time. But autologous exosomes, which are derived from your own blood, are the newer development everyone's talking about.

The results here have been genuinely promising, especially for hair density and regrowth. If you're exploring options at a hair clinic in Delhi for thinning hair or slow regrowth, this is one of the treatments worth asking your dermatologist about.

6. Touchless LHR (Laser Hair Reduction)

Laser hair reduction itself isn't new; most people are already familiar with it. But touchless LHR is a newer generation of devices that skips the gel application step entirely, making the whole process far less messy.

Beyond the convenience factor, the results tend to be more satisfying, and it works well across different hair and skin types, which is a genuine upgrade from some of the older devices still in use.

So, Which One Should You Actually Consider?

Here's the honest answer: it depends entirely on what you're trying to fix. Someone chasing glow and hydration is looking at a very different treatment path compared to someone dealing with hair thinning or someone wanting a non-surgical lift. This is exactly why a proper consultation matters more than following whatever's trending on social media that week.

At Dadu Medical Centre, led by Dr. Nivedita Dadu, these newer treatments are evaluated case by case rather than offered as one-size-fits-all packages. Whether you're exploring a skin clinic in Delhi for anti-ageing concerns or looking for a genuine skin specialist in Delhi to guide you through hair regrowth options, the right starting point is always an honest conversation about what your skin or scalp actually needs, not just what's popular this season.

It's also worth remembering that celebrity skincare treatment trends move fast, and not everything that works for someone else's skin type or budget will necessarily suit yours. A lot of what gets labelled as a celebrity beauty secret is really just a rebranded version of something dermatologists have used clinically for years, minus the glossy packaging.

If 2025 has taught us anything in this space, it's that skin and hair treatments are finally moving toward more personalised, less invasive, and longer-lasting solutions which is a genuinely good direction for anyone tired of quick fixes that don't last.
