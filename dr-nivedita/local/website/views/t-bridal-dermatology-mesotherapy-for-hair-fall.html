How Mesotherapy Works

Your scalp has a middle layer, the mesoderm, where the hair follicles get their blood supply. Mesotherapy places nutrients straight into that layer. Because they skip the digestive system, more of what's delivered reaches the follicles than you'd get from tablets or a topical product. The treatment also helps improve blood circulation in the scalp, which keeps supporting your hair in between sessions.

What Goes Into the Blend

There's no one standard formula. After looking at your scalp, the doctor puts together a mix that suits the reason your hair is falling. It can include:

  • vitamins such as biotin and B-complex
  • minerals like zinc and selenium
  • amino acids, the building blocks of keratin, which your hair is made of
  • growth factors or peptides that help stimulate follicles and reverse shrinking
  • in some cases, medication that works against DHT, a hormone linked to pattern hair loss

Needle or Needle-Free

There are a few ways to deliver the blend. Standard mesotherapy uses very fine needles to place small amounts across the scalp. Transdermal mesotherapy is needle-free and uses electroporation, which opens up the skin's water channels so the nutrients can pass through. MesoGrow delivers the blend using microneedling or electroporation. Your doctor will suggest which suits you.

Benefits

The main advantage is that the nutrients go where they're needed rather than being diluted through your whole body. The blend is built around your scalp, so it targets the actual reason behind your hair fall. If needles make you nervous, there's a needle-free option. Downtime is next to nothing, and mesotherapy works well alongside HGP, low-level laser or microneedling when your hair needs extra support.

Ideal Candidates

Mesotherapy is used for male pattern baldness, female pattern thinning, diffuse thinning and a receding hairline. It also helps with hair fall triggered by stress (telogen effluvium) or by nutritional deficiencies. It's suitable for both men and women, and the doctor assesses each case before finalising the plan, sometimes with blood tests.

Process & Techniques

A session takes about 30 to 60 minutes.

It starts with a scalp assessment, sometimes along with blood tests, so the doctor can work out what's driving your hair fall. Your scalp is cleaned, and the blend is prepared for you. It's then delivered by fine needle injections or by electroporation, depending on the method chosen. A short scalp massage may follow, and you'll get aftercare instructions before you leave. With needles, most people feel only mild discomfort. The needle-free options are painless.

How many sessions? Usually 6 to 10 for standard or transdermal mesotherapy, and 6 to 8 for MesoGrow. Sessions are spaced every two weeks or monthly, depending on how severe the hair fall is and how you respond. After the course, a maintenance session every one to three months helps hold the results.

What results look like: Shedding usually drops and hair starts to feel thicker after four to six sessions. A receding hairline may begin to respond within three or four. The best and longest-lasting results come once you've finished the full course.

Recovery: There's no real downtime. Your scalp may look slightly red or feel tender for a day or two.

Aftercare:

  • Don't wash your hair for at least 24 hours
  • Avoid heavy workouts and direct sun for a couple of days
  • Eat a balanced diet and drink enough fluids
  • Restart any prescribed hair serums two to three days after the session
