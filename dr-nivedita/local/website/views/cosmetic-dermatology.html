Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is cosmetic dermatology and how is it different from a regular facial at a salon?

Cosmetic dermatology uses medically proven treatments performed by qualified dermatologists including lasers, injectables, peels, and devices to address actual skin concerns, unlike salon treatments that only work on the surface.

What cosmetic skin treatments are available at Dadu Medical Centre in Delhi?

The clinic offers anti-wrinkle injections, fillers, HIFU, laser hair removal, acne scar treatments, pigmentation treatments, hair fall therapies, medifacials, thread lift, chemical peels, and many exclusive proprietary treatments.

Is cosmetic dermatology safe for Indian skin tones?

Yes, all treatments at the clinic are selected and calibrated specifically for Indian skin types. Dr. Nivedita Dadu has extensive experience treating patients across all skin tones safely.

Do I need a consultation before starting any cosmetic treatment?

Yes, every cosmetic treatment at Dadu Medical Centre begins with a proper skin assessment and consultation where the right treatment plan is built around the individual patient's specific concerns.

Can cosmetic treatments help with both acne scars and skin ageing at the same time?

Yes, some treatments like MNRF, Erbium Fibre Laser, and Fractional CO2 address both scars and ageing-related concerns together. The doctor recommends the best approach after examining the skin.

How many sessions of cosmetic treatment are typically needed?

The number of sessions varies by treatment and concern. Most laser and injectable treatments need three to eight sessions, while some procedures like fillers need only one per visit.

Are the results from cosmetic dermatology permanent?

Some treatments like laser hair removal provide long-term permanent reduction. Others like anti-wrinkle injections and fillers need periodic maintenance. Results from laser scar and pigmentation treatments are long-lasting with proper aftercare.

Can both men and women get cosmetic dermatology treatments?

Yes, all treatments at Dadu Medical Centre are available for both men and women. Protocols are adjusted based on individual skin type, concern, and desired outcome.

Which clinic in Delhi should I visit for cosmetic dermatology?

Dadu Medical Centre in Vasant Vihar and Rajouri Garden, led by Dr. Nivedita Dadu, is a trusted and comprehensive cosmetic dermatology clinic in Delhi offering the widest range of medical-grade aesthetic treatments.
