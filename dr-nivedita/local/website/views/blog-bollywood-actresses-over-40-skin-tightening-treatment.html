Why Skin Starts to Loosen After 40 — The Real Reasons

Understanding why skin laxity happens is the starting point for understanding why these treatments work. Here is what actually changes in the skin after 40:

Collagen Production Drops Dramatically After the age of 25 collagen production reduces by approximately 1 percent per year. By 40 you have lost roughly 15 percent of your skin's collagen. By 50 that figure is closer to 25 percent. Collagen is the structural protein that keeps skin firm, plump, and resilient; its loss is the primary driver of skin loosening.

Elastin Fibres Weaken Elastin gives skin its snap-back quality the ability to return to its original position after being stretched or moved. As elastin fibres weaken with age, skin loses this rebound quality and begins to sag and settle into a lower position.

Fat Pad Descent The fat pads that give the face its youthful fullness gradually shift downward with age. This causes hollowing in the upper face and accumulation of volume in the lower face contributing to jowling and a heavier lower facial appearance.

Bone Resorption The underlying facial bones actually shrink with age the jaw, cheekbones, and orbital bones all reduce in volume, removing the structural support that keeps the overlying skin and soft tissue in a youthful position.

Hormonal Changes After 40 The reduction in oestrogen that accompanies perimenopause and menopause directly affects skin quality reducing collagen synthesis, decreasing skin thickness, and accelerating the loss of firmness that would otherwise progress more slowly.

Gravity The most democratic force in the universe. It acts on facial tissue continuously and without mercy. Without the collagen and elastin scaffolding that kept skin in position during younger years, gravity progressively wins.

The Treatments That Are Changing Everything for Women Over 40

HIFU High Intensity Focused Ultrasound

HIFU is the closest thing to a surgical facelift that non-surgical medicine can currently offer. It delivers focused ultrasound energy to the deep structural layers of the skin, the same layers that a surgeon would address during a facelift stimulating intense collagen production and causing the deep tissue to contract and tighten.

The results develop progressively over 3 to 6 months as new collagen builds. A single session produces results that last 12 to 18 months for most patients. HIFU specifically addresses the deep SMAS layer, the muscular facial support system which no topical treatment or superficial procedure can reach. For actresses who need genuine deep tissue tightening without surgery, HIFU is the gold standard.

Radiofrequency Skin Tightening

Radiofrequency including technologies like Morpheus8, Thermage, and other RF devices delivers electrical energy into the dermis that heats the collagen fibres and causes them to contract and remodel. The immediate effect is mild tightening. The progressive effect over 3 to 6 months as new collagen forms is more significant tightening and firming of the skin surface and dermis.

Radiofrequency treatments are particularly effective for skin texture improvement, mild to moderate laxity, and as a maintenance treatment between more intensive procedures. Multiple sessions are typically recommended for best results and the treatment is well suited to combination with HIFU which addresses deeper tissue while radiofrequency works in the more superficial layers.

Thread Lifts

As discussed in the previous blog in this series, PDO and PLLA thread lifts provide immediate mechanical lifting combined with progressive collagen stimulation. For actresses over 40 dealing with jowling, descended cheeks, and lower face heaviness, thread lifts provide a more immediately dramatic lifting result than energy-based treatments alone.

Thread lifts work best in combination with HIFU or radiofrequency; the threads provide the immediate lift while the energy treatments progressively tighten and firm the surrounding skin to consolidate and extend the result.

Laser Skin Tightening and Resurfacing

Advanced laser treatments including fractional CO2 laser and non-ablative laser tightening address skin laxity through two mechanisms simultaneously. The heat energy contracts existing collagen for an immediate firming effect while the controlled skin injury triggers a wound healing response that produces significant new collagen over the following weeks and months.

Laser treatments are particularly effective for surface skin quality improving texture, reducing pigmentation, and firming the skin surface making them an excellent complement to the deeper tissue treatments that HIFU and thread lifts provide.
