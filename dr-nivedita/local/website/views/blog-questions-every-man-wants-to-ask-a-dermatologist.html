Questions every man wants to ask a dermatologist

Male skin is thicker, oilier, and has a rough texture compared to female skin. Male skin is approximately 25% thicker than female skin, and it contains less underlying fat and more collagen than female skin. Thicker skin appears more tightened, firmer, and plumper. In males, skin collagen content reduces at a constant rate, which makes it look dry and rough. Males must follow a proper skincare routine in order to maintain skin health. Males also have enlarged open pores because the sebaceous glands are more active, increasing the chances of sebum accumulation and further breakouts. The common signs of aging include sagging skin, puffy eyes, and dark circles. Often, it is observed that males hesitate in getting consultation from a dermatologist about the skincare routine. However, one must understand that skin problems and concerns have nothing to do with gender. Skincare and dermatology is not just for women.

So, here is an attempt to answer questions related skin and hair concerns that most men face.

How to get rid of back acne?

Often it is observed that males spend a lot of their time in the gym, which makes them look fit and muscular but at the same time can possibly worsen their skin health. The more men perspire; more they observe breakouts on their skin. Sweating is usually the reason for dirt, oil, and bacteria to accumulate in the skin, which further leads to breakouts on the face, back and arms. To prevent this, one must take a shower and change their clothes after a workout. Using a soap or gel containing acne-fighting ingredients like salicylic acid or benzoyl peroxide can be helpful. Following a healthy skincare routine, and dermatological treatment will be necessary to get rid of the existing acne, blackheads or whiteheads.

How to get rid of red bumps after shaving?

A good clean shave is the best thing to start the day off right. However, a common side effect or undesired result is razor burn or red bumps. This is an itchy and irritating skin condition, which is mainly caused by using a dull razor. This can be effectively treated by making exfoliation a part of the skincare routine. Proper shaving techniques must also be followed in order to avoid red bumps like shaving in the direction of hair growth to avoid friction and using a shaving cream. A gentle facial scrub helps slough off the dead skin cells and also brings ingrown hair out of hiding.

Is exfoliation important for male skin?

Exfoliation helps to get rid of the skin’s impurities. This will help take off the dead skin layer, which helps to create a brighter skin appearance. Exfoliation will also help improve the skin texture and make it easy for skin to absorb skincare products.

How to get rid of facial lines?

Men are usually concerned about smile lines, forehead lines, and crow’s feet. Everyone wants to look their best and have a younger-looking appearance. Many cosmetic treatments are available, which reduce the appearance of aging lines and provide a rejuvenated look. Men can rely on cosmetic treatments such as dermal fillers, anti-wrinkle injections, thread lift, and laser resurfacing to get a youthful look. Following a proper, healthy skincare regime will help in delaying the signs of aging. Seeking the help of a dermatologist is the first step towards attaining healthy skin. Everybody’s skin is different, so different treatments and skincare routines must be followed to have healthy skin.
