How a Carbon Laser Peel Works

The treatment does a few jobs in one sitting, and they happen in a particular order.

First, a thin layer of liquid carbon is spread over the skin and left to settle into the pores. Carbon is good at soaking up oil and impurities, so it pulls in what's sitting inside them. Then the laser (the clinic uses the Alma Q laser) is passed over the area. It targets the carbon particles and breaks them apart, and the oil and debris they absorbed go with them.

That same pass also lifts away dead skin cells, which leaves skin feeling softer and pores looking smaller. Meanwhile, the gentle heat reaches deeper layers and encourages collagen, which can help skin look a bit firmer and brighter over a course of sessions.

What It Helps With

Most people come in for oily skin, blackheads, clogged or enlarged pores and dullness. It's also used alongside acne care. The heat may help reduce the bacteria linked to acne and calm down oil production for a while, so pores don't congest as quickly.

It isn't just a face treatment either. The back and chest, which often get oily and break out, can be treated too.

Process & Techniques

  • Skin assessment: A clinician checks your skin to make sure a carbon peel is the right fit.
  • Carbon layer: Liquid carbon is applied in a thin coat and left to settle into the pores.
  • Laser pass: The Alma Q laser is moved over the area, breaking up the carbon along with the oil and debris it has absorbed. Most people feel only mild warmth.
  • Finish: The whole session takes around 30 to 45 minutes.

How many sessions? Skin usually looks better after the first one. For longer-lasting results, a course of four to six sessions is typical. Results vary from person to person.

Aftercare: Treated skin is more sensitive for a while, so wear sunscreen every day to protect it and keep your results.

FAQs

What is a carbon laser peel, and why is it called the Hollywood peel?

It's a laser treatment that deep-cleans pores, exfoliates and refreshes the skin. The nickname comes from how quickly skin looks brighter afterwards.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel only mild warmth. There are no needles involved.

How many sessions will I need?

You'll usually notice a difference after the first session. A course of four to six gives longer-lasting results.

Can it help with both acne and oily skin?

It can help. The heat may reduce acne-causing bacteria and calm oil production for a while.

Is it safe for Indian skin?

When it's done by experienced doctors using the right settings, it's generally well suited to Indian skin. Your doctor will check your skin first.

Can I get my back or chest treated?

Yes. Carbon peel works on any oily or acne-prone area, not just the face.

How long does a session take?

Around 30 to 45 minutes.

Do I need sun protection afterwards?

Yes. Freshly treated skin is more sensitive, so daily sunscreen helps protect it and keep your results.
