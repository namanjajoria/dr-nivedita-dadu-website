Sagging Skin

Sagging skin is a condition in which your skin looks loose. It does not remain tight as in the young age. It’s a normal part of aging and affects everyone with time. Sagging skin may cause jowl fullness over the mid-jawline as the lower cheek fat descends to increase volume where you don’t want it, like in the jowl area, in the neck and above the nasolabial folds.
