What Causes Pigmentation — And Why Indian Skin Is Particularly Affected

Before getting into the celebrity stories it helps to understand what pigmentation actually is and why it is so prevalent among Indian women including those in Bollywood whose skin is under constant professional scrutiny.

Pigmentation occurs when melanin the pigment that gives skin its colour is produced unevenly or excessively in certain areas. The result is patches or spots of skin that are darker than the surrounding tone. There are several distinct types and each has a slightly different cause and a slightly different treatment approach.

Melasma The most common and most stubborn form of pigmentation in Indian women. It appears as symmetrical patches of darker skin typically across the cheeks, upper lip, forehead, and bridge of the nose. It is driven primarily by hormonal changes pregnancy, oral contraceptives, and hormonal fluctuations all trigger melasma combined with sun exposure which dramatically worsens it. Indian skin is particularly prone to melasma because of its higher baseline melanin content which makes it more reactive to hormonal and UV triggers.

Sun Spots and Solar Lentigines These are the flat dark spots that develop on areas of the skin chronically exposed to UV radiation the face, hands, décolletage, and shoulders. In Delhi's high UV environment, significant sun damage accumulates over years of daily outdoor exposure even in women who are careful about sun protection.

Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation This is the dark mark left behind after acne, a wound, a rash, or any inflammatory skin event heals. Indian skin is particularly prone to PIH because its high melanin reactivity means the inflammation response almost always involves excess melanin production at the site. This is why Bollywood actresses who have had acne in their younger years often deal with residual marks long after the active acne has resolved.

Uneven Skin Tone This is a generalised unevenness of skin colour that is not localised to specific spots but creates an overall patchy, dull, or inconsistent appearance. It is caused by a combination of sun damage, melanin irregularity, and environmental factors and is one of the most common reasons women in Delhi seek professional treatment.

Pigmentation Treatment Options What Bollywood Actresses Are Using

Chemical Peels

Chemical peels use carefully formulated acid solutions applied to the skin surface to remove the outer layers of dead and pigmented skin cells and stimulate the production of new, evenly pigmented skin beneath. Different peel depths superficial, medium, and deep address different levels of pigmentation severity. For Bollywood actresses with busy schedules, superficial peels with essentially no downtime are the most practical option for regular maintenance.

Laser Pigmentation Treatment

Q-switched laser, Nd:YAG laser, and fractional laser treatments target melanin specifically breaking up pigment deposits into particles small enough for the body's natural processes to remove. Laser treatment in Delhi for pigmentation has become increasingly sophisticated with modern devices able to treat pigmentation at specific depths without affecting surrounding tissue. Results are typically visible from the first session with optimal results achieved over a course of 4 to 6 treatments.

Chemical Peel Combined With Laser

The combination of chemical peels and laser treatment produces results that neither achieves as effectively alone. Peels prepare the skin surface and improve penetration of laser energy while laser treats deeper pigment deposits that peels cannot reach. This combination approach is the most commonly recommended protocol at premium skin clinics in Delhi for significant melasma or deep hyperpigmentation.

Medical Grade Skincare

No pigmentation treatment programme is complete without the right topical support. Medical-grade skincare prescribed by a dermatologist in Delhi containing active ingredients like vitamin C, niacinamide, kojic acid, azelaic acid, and prescription-strength retinoids maintains the results of clinical treatments between sessions and provides daily protection against new pigmentation formation.

Cosmelan Depigmentation Peel

Cosmelan is a professional depigmentation treatment that combines a clinic-applied peel mask with an intensive home care protocol. It is one of the most effective treatments available for melasma and widespread hyperpigmentation and produces dramatic results over a course of several weeks. Its results on darker Indian skin tones are particularly well-documented and it is used extensively at premium aesthetic clinics in Delhi for significant pigmentation cases.
