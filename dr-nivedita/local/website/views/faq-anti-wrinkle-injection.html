1. HOW DOES ANTI-WRINKLE INJECTION WORK?

Anti-wrinkle injection consists of a neuromodulator that is injected into the targeted treatment area through an ultra-fine needle. It works by blocking the nerve signals required for facial muscles to contract. It’s the repetitive contraction of facial muscles that causes expression lines and wrinkles to appear. Once the neuromodulator is injected it relaxes the muscles and decreases their movement. Hence, diminishing the appearance of fine lines or wrinkles. You will see results gradually over 3-7 days following anti-wrinkle injection treatment in Delhi at Dr. Nivedita Dadu’s Dermatology Clinic.

2. WHICH AREAS OF THE FACE CAN BE TREATED WITH ANTI-WRINKLE INJECTION?

Anti-wrinkle injection in Delhi is used to decrease fine lines and wrinkles on different facial areas including the forehead, eye area (crow’s feet), and lip lines. It is also injected to soften the dimpling of the chin. Hence, it is one of the best anti-aging treatments in Delhi.
It can also be injected into the muscles of the jaw to help alleviate pain from teeth grinding or TMJ (Temporomandibular joint) or for a jaw slimming effect.

5. HOW SOON WILL I SEE THE RESULTS?

While the experience of each patient may differ, it usually takes 3-5 days for the neuromodulators to settle down. For some patients, it may take up to 2 weeks to realize the final effects of this anti wrinkle treatment in Delhi.

6. HOW LONG DOES ANTI-WRINKLE INJECTION LAST?

The results of anti-wrinkle injection typically sustain between 3-5 months from the date of injection. With time the body metabolizes the injected neuromodulator, leading to a slower return of muscle movement in the treatment area. Patients can choose to sustain their anti-wrinkle injection results for longer periods by going through periodic maintenance treatments every 3-4 months. Yes, anti-wrinkle injection is an FDA (Food and Drug Administration) approved treatment for reversing fine lines and wrinkles. 9. WHAT SHOULD YOU DO TO PREPARE FOR THE TREATMENT?

A week before the treatment most aesthetic doctors in Delhi will advise their patients to stop consuming alcohol or taking medications or supplements that thin the blood like ibuprofen, Gingko Biloba, or Ginseng. This will help in minimizing the bruising after the procedure.

10. ARE THERE ANY SIDE EFFECTS?

The side effects may include bruising, swelling, redness, or tenderness around the injection area, all of which will resolve without intervention after a few days to a week.
