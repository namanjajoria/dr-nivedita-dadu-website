Dermaroller - The secret to get younger looking skin

Dermaroller is a handheld device with microneedles. There are various indications in which a dermaroller treatment has been proven effective.

So, why choose Dermaroller?

  • Diminishing wrinkles & fine lines: Wrinkles and sun damage are mainly caused due to the loss of collagen & elastin. Dermaroller significantly improves the skin condition by inducing more collagen production.
  • Treating the scars: Dermaroller benefits the skin by reducing various types of marks such as acne scars, surgical scars, and even stretch marks.
  • Eliminating pigmentation: Microneedling reduces hyperpigmentation by shedding of the top dermal layer of the skin and gives your skin a new refreshed look.
  • Removing age spots: Dermaroller is an effective technique which not only helps in minimizing the age spots but also aids in removing the upper layer of the skin.

After reading all these benefits, we are sure you would want to know how actually this procedure is performed. So, here we go with all the details:

Before the procedure
Before starting the procedure, a topical numbing gel is applied to the treatment area for optimal comfort of the patient.

During the procedure
The procedure begins with the rolling of the device on the target area gently. Then the microneedles of dermaroller, create pores in the skin, which encourage the release of growth factors. The growth factors, in turn, generate a wound healing cascade by stimulating collagen production.

Use of Vital Serums
At Dr. Nivedita Dadu’s Dermatology Clinic in West Delhi, we have an additional step to this procedure which is the application of vital serums. These serums are applied to the treated area, basis your specific skin concerns. For e.g.

  • Hyaluronic acid growth factors and skin boosters for anti-aging
  • Vitamin C serums and skin enhancers for facial rejuvenation as well as glow
  • Vitamin E and rosehip oils for stretch marks
  • Collagen and elastin containing serums for acne scars

Post-procedure care
Once the procedure is complete your skin specialist will guide you about the skincare regimen you should follow to attain optimum results.

  • Strict avoidance of sunlight
  • Regular application of sunscreens
  • Using mild soap/facial cleanser during the initial days

How to choose the right Dermaroller for you?

The dermaroller comes in different sizes and what kind of dermaroller you would require for your skin rejuvenation can be decided only after an assessment by the dermatologist.
