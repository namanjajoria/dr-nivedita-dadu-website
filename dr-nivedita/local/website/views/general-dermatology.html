Inside a Consultation

Inside a consultation

The consultation process at Dadu Medical Centre is thorough and structured in a way that puts the patient's understanding first. Dr. Nivedita Dadu and her team of qualified skin specialists in Delhi believe strongly that a patient who understands their condition is better able to follow the treatment plan and get the best results.

The consultation begins with a detailed discussion of the patient's concern when it started, what makes it better or worse, what has already been tried, and what other health conditions or medications the patient has. This is followed by a physical examination of the affected skin, hair, or nails. Where needed, diagnostic tests including blood tests to check for hormonal imbalances, nutritional deficiencies, thyroid function, or other underlying causes may be recommended.

Once the root cause is identified, a treatment plan is discussed with the patient that may include prescription medications topical or oral along with lifestyle and dietary guidance, and in-clinic procedures if needed. Follow-up appointments are scheduled to monitor progress and adjust the treatment plan as the condition responds.

Skin conditions that are chronic such as psoriasis, eczema, rosacea, or melasma require long-term management and the doctors at the clinic guide patients through this ongoing care with clear, practical advice.
