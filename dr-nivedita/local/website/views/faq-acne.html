If you are suffering only from mild acne and your skin exhibits just occasional breakouts, count on anti-acne products. However, if your skin shows any of the following warning signs, make an immediate appointment with a skin care professional or physician:

  • Acne has catalyzed due to new medication prescription
  • Experiencing severe emotional distress

  • Encountering other physical symptoms apart from acne

2. What foods cause acne?

The diet you follow says a lot about your skin almost just like your waistline. Reason being, the food you eat is directly proportional to your health. Certain foods raise your blood sugar comparatively fast than others. When there is a rise in blood sugar, there is the rise in sugar hormone. Excess blood sugar results in more oil production from oil glands and increased risk to acne. Few common foods that trigger a spike in sugar are as follows:

  • pasta
  • white rice
  • white bread
  • sugar

All these foods are known as “high-glycemic” carbohydrates because of their sugar-producing effects. Indeed, chocolate also has potential to worsen acne. Fortunately, it does not affect all people. In addition to these, several other pieces of research show the connection between acne and so-called Western Diet and Standard American Diet. This diet is based heavily on:

  • Dairy
  • Trans fats
  • Saturated fats
  • High- glycemic carbohydrates

Consequently, results in increased acne risk. According to reported researches, these kinds of foods stimulate the production of hormones that can cause excess oil to be created and secreted by oil glands. Further, it is also found that it is the Western diet that is linked to greater inflammation and adversely impact to acne problems. 9. How can I stop getting pimples on my face ?

Trust Low-glycemic diets.

According to several recent studies, the sure-shot way to prevent as well as improve acne is to follow a low-glycemic diet or a diet that is low in simple sugars. A study performed on Korean patients by researchers also proves a significant improvement in acne. In this study, Korean patients followed a low-glycemic load for 10 weeks, and results surprised the users. Not only this, another study published in the Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology, also confirms this. This study proves that the men, who followed a low-glycemic, high-protein diet for 12 weeks found significant improvement in acne and weight loss.

Low level of Vitamin A and E are the common factors for severe acne. To reduce the acne severity, consumption of foods that contain these vitamins is a great recommendation. However, it is always advisable to contact your doctor first before consuming Vitamin A supplements. Reason being, Vitamin A toxicity can result in permanent damage to major organs.

Omega-3s and antioxidants are a perfect amalgamation to reduce inflammation.
