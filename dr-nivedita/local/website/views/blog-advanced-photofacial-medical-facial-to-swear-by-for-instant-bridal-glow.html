Advanced Photofacial - A medical facial to swear by for instant bridal glow

Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Dermatology Clinic offers a special procedure for all our brides to be who want an instant glow for their D-Day.

Are you tired of trying out all the facials at the salon repeatedly and still not getting the glow you desire? At Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Dermatology Clinic we can proudly put an end to your disappointment with our signature Advanced Photofacial promising an instant glow to our brides.

Let's first brief you about the procedure of Advanced Photofacial which initially requires you to consult the dermatologist, Dr. Nivedita Dadu for an assessment of your skin and to discuss your goals and expectations. An Advanced Photofacial is an hour-long treatment, which includes the following steps: The first step is the exfoliation of dead skin cells, closing of open pores, removal of blackheads and whiteheads; and polishing to prepare the skin for our further steps. The next step is unique to our advanced treatment and includes the application of 'Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Signature Power Glow Serum' with skin whitening boosters, on the prepped up skin. This is followed by the application of an anti-oxidant face pack that gives an instant glow to your skin. The last step is the use of different modes of IPL laser the energy settings of which will be decided by the doctor during your consultation as it may differ on different skin types. So, if you notice all the steps in this procedure mainly focus on bringing out the healthy inner glow on your face. And because it starts with a thorough examination of your skin, you don't have to worry about the side effects or the final results for that matter.

Our Advanced Photofacial is, therefore, way ahead of the regular facials in different spas and skin clinics you may have visited. It has been a major hit with our patients who are beaming with the results of our Advanced Photofacials. So, brides come up and shine through your D day with your clear & radiant skin!
