1. HOW OFTEN SHOULD I GET FACIALS?

It is recommended to take facial atleast once a month as it help in the cell turn over process which happens approximately once in a every month. As we grow old, this cell turn over process slows down, contributing to dull sallow looking skin, fine lines, wrinkles, blackheads, congestion and roughly textured skin. Monthly facials assist your body in doing more efficiently what it is “supposed" to do on its own.

2. WHAT CAN I EXPECT DURING A FACIAL TREATMENT?

When you will arrive at the clinic, you will be asked to fill out a consultation form. Dr. Nivedita will analyse your skin and discuss your skin concerns. All facials are uniquely customized to target your specific skincare needs.

3. WILL MY SKIN BE RED OR IRRITATED AFTER MY FACIAL?

Slight redness is normal after a facial, especially if you've received any sort of chemical exfoliant such as a glycolic or an alpha hydroxy acid, or received a large amount of extractions. You may experience Slight irritation during these instances, however it should not continue after your facial treatment has been completed. People, who tend to have more sensitive skin, may be prone to experiencing more redness post treatment.

4. HOW CAN I MAINTAIN PROFESSIONAL SKINCARE RESULTS AT HOME ON A DAILY BASIS?

Dermalogica offers a wide range of exceptional, at home skincare products available for purchase. Not sure which product is right for you? After facial analysis, Dr. Nivedita can guide you to choose the best Dermalogica products according to your skin need & concerns.

5. CAN I RECEIVE A FACIAL WAXING SUCH AS AN EYEBROW OR LIP WAX ON THE SAME DAY AS MY FACIAL?

Dermalogica facials are medicated and incorporate mid exfoliation of dead cells with chemical exfoliants like alpha hydroxyl acids. Therefore waxing should be done, few days before or after your facial, for the up most comfort and safety of your skin and to avoid any further irritation of the skin.

6. DO MEN GET FACIALS TOO?

Absolutely! Facials have many advantages for men too, and can help lessen skin sensitivity from shaving, as well as other concerns from lack of proper at home skincare. If you suffer from sensitivity after shaving, it is suggested that you should not shave immediately after your facial, as this can lead to further irritation.
