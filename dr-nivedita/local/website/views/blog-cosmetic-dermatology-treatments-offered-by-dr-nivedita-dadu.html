Cosmetic Dermatology Treatments Offered By Dr.Nivedita Dadu

Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Dermatology Clinic is one of the Best Cosmetic Dermatology & Diode Laser Hair Removal Clinics in Delhi. Under cosmetic dermatology Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Dermatology Clinic provides a vast array of services focusing on the desired look and appearance of a patient. We follow a multi-dimensional approach to all skin related problems which means that we take care of diagnostics, medical treatments and aesthetic appeal of each and every treatment. Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Dermatology Clinic is known for providing highest qualities of laser hair removal and various anti-ageing treatments in Delhi. As we already mentioned, the treatments at Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Dermatology Clinic are very result-oriented and not only provides a healthy & younger looking skin but also aims to enhance overall customer experience.

Various cosmetic dermatological treatments are used to treat blemishes, improve skin tone, and give you a desired appearance. The transformation of skin enables you to be more confident and helps you look like the best version of you. Following is a list of treatments that we perform at our clinic.

Stretch Marks are scars which also mean discoloration of skin due to puberty, pregnancy, excessive exercising etc. Stretch marks are treated by using Collagen Induction therapy & scar treatment laser.

Periorbital Pigmentation is the medical term for dark circles and is healed with the techniques like iPeel, Eye filler and laser toning.

Acne and Acne Scars occur most commonly during teenage because of the hormonal changes. Our estheticians suggest teenage peel, acne clean up, collagen induction therapy or scar treatment laser to get rid of acne and its scars.

Anti Ageing treatmentssuch as age reverse peel, age lock facial, age preserve laser, anti wrinkle injections, fillers, vital, age freeze technology and thread lift help you delaying the natural ageing process.

Pigmentation on skin is caused due to over production of melanin and makes your skin look dull & patchy. Adjuvant therapies like chemical peels, meso facials, insta-glow peel facials and laser toning helps in reduction of pigmentation and keep them at bay.

Hair Fall Management at Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Dermatology Clinic starts with finding the underlying cause with the help of blood tests to understand the deficiencies and treat it accordingly. We use mesotherapy and platlet rich plasma therapy to manage excessive hair fall.

Laser Hair Reduction is comparatively a new treatment as compared to shaving, waxing & plucking so as to get rid of unwanted hair. Laser hair removal treatment works fast, is painless and provides long lasting solution to unwanted hair.

Hair Transplant treatment involves plucking follicles from the back of the head and implanted at the bald area of the head. This technique is world’s most advanced hair implant treatment and is known as ” Direct in ” FUE technique. Upto 5000 hair can be implanted in one sitting. It takes almost 6 months for the results to be visible.
