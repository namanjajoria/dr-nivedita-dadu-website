All About Face-Lift: Thread Lift Secrets with Dr. Nodo Mdivani & Dr. Nandini Dadu

We recently sat down for a special episode at Dadu Medical Centre with Dr. Nodo Mdivani, a plastic surgeon from Georgia with over five years of experience and 30 years of collective thread lift expertise behind the brand she works with. Hosted by Dr. Nandini Dadu, this conversation turned into one of the most detailed, no-fluff breakdowns of thread lifts we've put together covering everything from face to body, and answering the exact questions patients bring up in consultations every single week. If you've been curious about non-surgical treatment in Delhi for sagging skin, this one's worth reading in full.

Why Most Patients Skip Threads Without Even Knowing It

One of the first things Dr. Dadu raised in the conversation was a pattern she sees constantly in her own practice. A patient walks in with sagging jowls, deep nasolabial folds, drooping eyebrows, or a jawline that's lost its definition. The conversation usually goes something like this: they'll first ask about RF or HIFU technology, not realising these energy-based devices won't do much for genuine skin laxity. Then they get nervous about fillers or Botox because "I'm too young for injectables." By the time threads come up as an option, they suddenly feel it's "too invasive." And ironically, some of these same patients would happily consider a full surgical facelift before trying a thread lift which, when you think about it, doesn't really make sense.

Dr. Mdivani agreed this isn't unique to India either. It's a global pattern, and it usually comes down to a lack of clear information about what thread lifts actually do.

So What Exactly Are Thread Lifts?

Thread lifting has come a long way since it first started decades ago. What began as a fairly basic concept has evolved through multiple generations of technology, with the newer generations built using materials like hyaluronic acid combined into the thread itself. This isn't just about lifting sagging tissue anymore; patients also notice a genuine improvement in overall skin quality within a few weeks of treatment.

One of the biggest advantages over fillers, Dr. Mdivani explained, is how natural the result looks. With fillers, there's sometimes that slightly "puffy" look right after the procedure. Threads don't do that. The results settle in gradually and look far more like your own face, just lifted.

How Long Do the Results Actually Last?

This came up early in the discussion, and it's a fair question given how often fillers and Botox need repeating. With a good thread lift, results can genuinely last between one and a half to two years, sometimes longer, depending on the area treated and how the patient's tissue responds. There's minimal fibrotic reaction with newer thread compositions, which is part of why the results tend to hold up so well over time.

Recovery is refreshingly quick too. Most patients deal with two to three days of mild swelling, and after that, there's no redness, no visible asymmetry, and nothing that stops them from getting back to normal life, something Dr. Mdivani noted matters especially to Indian patients balancing busy schedules.

Not a One-Size-Fits-All Treatment

What became clear during this discussion is that thread selection is highly individualised. There isn't a single universal thread that works for every face or every concern; the right approach depends on how much sagging is present, whether there's excess skin, fat pad involvement, or a combination of issues. Sometimes a single thread type does the job; other times, combining different thread types or even combining threads with Botox gives better results, especially in the upper face where muscle movement plays a bigger role in how folds form.

For deep nasolabial folds specifically, nano threads have become a popular minimally invasive option they help fill and support the area from within, and results tend to firm up further over a couple of weeks as the tissue responds.

What About the Body? Not Just a Facial Treatment Anymore

This is where the conversation got particularly interesting, especially for patients who've lost significant weight and are dealing with loose skin on the arms, abdomen, or inner thighs. Body threads work on a similar principle: a crossing technique is used with small entry points, no cutting involved, and the thread sits in the subcutaneous layer. Recovery here is just as quick, with swelling settling within two to three days.

For patients who've lost a substantial amount of weight through diet, weight loss injections, or lifestyle changes and are still struggling with skin laxity despite toning exercises, this can be a genuinely effective option worth discussing with your dermatologist.

Body Area Suitable For Recovery Time
Inner Arms Skin laxity after weight loss 2–3 days
Abdomen Mild to moderate laxity 2–3 days
Inner Thighs Post weight-loss sagging 2–3 days
Breast Area Mild ptosis (best in lighter tissue) Up to 1.5 years in ideal candidates

Is It Ever Combined With Other Treatments?

Quite often, yes. For patients with heavier tissue in the lower face or double chin concerns, Dr. Mdivani mentioned it sometimes makes more sense to first do a bit of fat reduction or lipolytic treatment, wait a few weeks, and then follow up with threads for a cleaner, more defined result. Trying to do everything in a single sitting isn't always the smarter route, even though most patients understandably want quick results.

Is a Thread Lift Right for You?

By the end of the session, one thing was clear: candidacy depends entirely on your specific concern, skin thickness, and how much sagging you're dealing with. That's exactly why an in-person evaluation matters so much before deciding on any aesthetic treatment in Delhi. A quick pinch test, a tissue assessment, and an honest conversation with your dermatologist about expectations usually make all the difference between an average result and one that genuinely looks natural.

At Dadu Medical Centre, thread lift candidacy is assessed individually rather than treated as a blanket solution because what works beautifully for one patient's jawline might not be the right call for someone else's nasolabial folds or body laxity. If you're exploring options at a skin clinic in Delhi for sagging skin, whether on your face or body, it's worth having this conversation with a qualified skin specialist in Delhi before deciding on your next step.
