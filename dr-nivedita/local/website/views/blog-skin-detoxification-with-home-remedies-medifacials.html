Skin Detoxification with Home Remedies & Medifacials

Almost every day we face various environmental stresses that can exhaust skin and clog pores leading to various skin concerns like inflammation, redness, itching, and irritation. Environmental stress and pollution can also accelerate the ageing process and can result in skin issues like wrinkles, pigmentation, dryness and acne. With the busy schedules and fast routines the major challenge we face today is shortage of time to get professional help to detoxify the skin and help it protect and repair from the external stress.

Modern lifestyle increases our exposure to UV damages and pollution. All of these factors create havoc on the healthy skin and wreck it up to the core. Naturally, our body does have the tendency to activate lymphatic system and efficiently clear out toxins and impurities by flushing them out of the body and self repair the skin. However, at times, unhealthy lifestyle and reduced physical activity tend to block the lymphatic system from performing its functions properly and hence, resulting in toxin build up that causes various skin issues.

Skin detoxification is one of the options that can help the skin breathe better and start afresh by encouraging removal of impurities and increasing collagen and elastin production. Detox procedures that help in increasing skin cell renewal and cell turnover rate will improve the strength of skin barrier and help skin cells revitalize and make the skin glow.

Skin Detox at Home
There are ready ingredients available around us that have strong detoxification properties. These ingredients can be used in daily skincare routine to help detoxify the skin and make it healthy and glowing. Some of them are:

  1. Mushrooms
    This is a new trending skincare ingredient which has the capability to adapt to changes and restore the balance. These consist of around 400 bioactive compounds making their regenerative capacity more than any other ingredient. It is a great antioxidant and has anti-inflammatory properties which are beneficial for skin as well as body.

  2. Microgreens
    These are the miniature plants which include radish, sprouts, broccoli which consist of six times more nutrients than any other mature plants. They are brimmed up with vitamins, minerals and other micronutrients that help boost skin health. Studies have proven that these nutrients are extremely important to maintain skin health and overall health.

  3. Birch water
    Birch tree is known to be the powerhouse of electrolytes, flavanoids, proteins, enzymes, vitamins and amino acids. Birch water has ability to replenish the skin and keep it healthy. It can also be consumed as a beverage. Alternatively, it is used as strong hydrating agent with antioxidant properties in skincare products.

  4. Vitamin C
    It is a famous skincare ingredient as it is a potent antioxidant that is essential for skin cell renewal, its development and repair. Vitamin C is an all rounder saviour for skin that protects it from UV damage, reduces pigmentation, inflammation and helps boost collagen and elastin production. Adding a surge of Vitamin C based skincare products ensures glowing healthy skin.

  5. Clay
    It helps remove the impurities from skin. It is commonly used as a face mask that removes excess oil and draws out impurities from the face. It helps in pulling the toxins away and unclogging the pores.
