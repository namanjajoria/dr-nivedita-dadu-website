FAQs

Schedule a Consultation

1. What skin conditions does it help with?

MARTINI is a treatment which is tailored to suit a vast array of skin conditions, like dullness, tanning, pigmentation, uneven skin tone, dryness, sun damage and pigmentation, dehydration and dull appearance.

2. Is there any downtime?

With No downtime, MARTINI is a treatment which allows you to return to your normal daily activities immediately. The safety of the treatment means that we can also treat the tired under eye area and dry, thinning lips as well

3. Is it painful?

Though this treatment is vacuum suction and slight needling based but to everyone’s surprise it is a completely comfortable and pain free experience.

4. How many treatments will I need?

For best results we recommend successive sessions that can vary from patient to patient depending on their skin type and concern.

5. What is the pre and post treatment care?

We recommend avoiding the use of any exfoliative skin care products for 1 day pre and 3 days post treatment, such as retinol, lactic acid, glycolic acid etc. For optimal results we recommend you follow a daily skin care regime alongside treatments. Follow strict photoprotection after the treatment.

6. Are there any contraindications to the treatment?

We do not recommend MARTINI treatment for those with severe acne, broken or inflamed skin; and those with Epilepsy.

7. Can I do gyming and exercise after treatment?

Yes, it is completely safe to do gym and exercise 24 hours after treatment.

8. What all conditions can be treated with MARTINI?

MARTINI hydration and MARTINI Glow are the two treatment options. MARTINI helps in working on pigmentation and dark spots by clearing and cleansing blocked oily pores, and killing bacteria, for clear and fresh skin and also evens out pigmentation marks, by lightening the skin. It treats dryness with the help of a unique formulation of serums which helps to deliver high levels of water to the deeper layers of the skin through the micro-channeling technique. This may result in hydrating and smoothing the skin.

9. Can I attend party after the treatment?

Yes, Martini does not have any downtime. And you can apply make up also after 4-6 hours time. So it is completely safe to attend party after the treatment
