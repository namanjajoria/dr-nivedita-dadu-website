Why We Pair Microdermabrasion With the Peel

A peel on its own is a bit hit and miss. If the skin is covered in dead, flaky cells, the peel sits on top in some places and sinks in at others, and the result comes out uneven.

Doing microdermabrasion first sorts that out. With the dead layer gone, the brightening actives land on live skin and get to work on melanin (the pigment behind tan and dark spots). The same-day glow? That's mostly the exfoliation. Actual lightening happens slowly, over a few sittings.

Concerns Power Glow Can Help With

People usually come in for Power Glow because of dull skin or a tan that just won't go. It helps with uneven tone too, and with light dark spots and those brown marks old pimples leave. In most cases mild spots look noticeably lighter by the third or fourth session. Tan fades sooner.

Brides mostly want one thing though. Skin that looks fresh and holds makeup well, and this does that.

Process & Techniques

It takes about 30 to 45 minutes. Roughly, this is how it goes:

  • Your face is cleansed so no makeup, oil or dirt is left behind.
  • Next comes microdermabrasion with a diamond-tip device. It lifts off dead cells while a gentle suction picks up the debris.
  • A moisturiser goes on. Skin shouldn't feel stripped going into the peel.
  • Then the whitening peel spreads evenly over the face.
  • It stays on for a set time and is taken off carefully.
  • Sunscreen lasts, because freshly treated skin shouldn't meet the sun unprotected.

How many sessions? Depends on what you're after. One or two usually does it for a single event. Pigmentation needs more patience, typically four to six sessions with a gap of two to four weeks in between. You can also book a single sitting, a basic course (3 to 4), a standard course (4 to 6), a bridal package (6 to 8) or monthly maintenance every 3 to 4 weeks. Your dermatologist will suggest what fits.

Aftercare: Sunscreen daily, SPF 45 or above, no excuses. Stay out of harsh sun for a few days if you can, or carry a hat or umbrella. Don't scrub or use exfoliating products for a little while. A simple moisturiser is enough.

FAQs

What is Power Glow treatment and how does it work?

It's a skin brightening treatment that first removes dead cells with microdermabrasion, then applies a whitening peel to fresh skin. You get an instant glow, and pigmentation reduces gradually over sessions.

Is there any downtime?

None. Most people go straight back to their day and can even wear makeup the same evening.

How many sessions will I need?

One or two sessions are usually enough for an event to glow. For pigmentation correction, four to six sessions spaced two to four weeks apart work best.

Is Power Glow good for brides and grooms?

Yes. It was designed with them in mind, and many couples take it as part of their pre-wedding skin plan.

Does it work on Indian skin?

Yes, it's suitable for all Indian skin tones.

Can it remove sun tan?

It helps a lot. The peel works on the surface melanin that builds up from sun exposure, so tan starts lightening from the early sessions.

What should I do after the treatment?

Wear SPF 45 or higher daily, avoid direct sun for a few days, skip harsh scrubs, and keep the skin moisturised.
