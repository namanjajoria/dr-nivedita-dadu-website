How Soprano ICE Works

Soprano ICE, made by Alma Lasers, uses a method called SHR (Super Hair Removal). Rather than one strong flash, it fires many quick, low-energy pulses that build heat in the follicle slowly. The follicle gets hot enough to be damaged, but the skin doesn't take a sudden jolt.

Two features make this comfortable. ICE cooling keeps the surface of the skin cool throughout, which helps limit discomfort and surface burns. The In-Motion technique means the handpiece glides continuously over the area instead of stamping spot by spot, so coverage is even and nothing gets overheated.

The device carries three wavelengths, each reaching a different depth in the skin:

WavelengthSkin typesBest suited for
Alexandrite 755nmI to IVlighter, finer hair
Diode 810nmI to Vlarger areas
Nd:YAG 1064nmI to VIdark, coarse hair and darker skin

They can be used together in a combined "Trio" mode. Your specialist decides the combination after looking at your skin and hair.

Areas That Can Be Treated

Legs, arms, underarms, back, chest, stomach and the bikini or Brazilian area are all common. On the face, Soprano ICE is used for the upper lip, chin, cheeks, neck and beard line. A smaller facial tip handles the precise work, like shaping eyebrows or the beard, and removing ear or nostril hair. Full body packages are available too.

For the groin area, a disposable tip is used for hygiene, and numbing cream is applied beforehand. The disposable tip is charged separately.

Ideal Candidates

Anyone who wants long-term hair reduction but has been nervous about pain is a good fit. Process & Techniques

  • Assessment: Your skin type, hair colour and thickness are checked, and the wavelength or combination is chosen.
  • Prep: Shave the area beforehand. For the bikini area, numbing cream is applied at the clinic.
  • Treatment: The cooled handpiece glides over the skin continuously. Small areas like the upper lip take a few minutes, while full legs or the back take around 45 to 60 minutes.
  • After: Some mild redness may show up and usually settles within a few hours. Treated hair starts shedding over the next two to three weeks.

How many sessions? Most body areas need 6 to 8 sessions, spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. Hormonal areas take longer, and the upper lip can need 6 to 10. A yearly touch-up may help keep results going. Progress usually looks something like this, though it varies with skin type, hair colour, area and hormones:

  • Session 1: around 10 to 20% less hair, with some shedding in 2 to 3 weeks
  • Sessions 2 to 3: around 30 to 50%, with finer, slower regrowth
  • Sessions 4 to 5: around 50 to 70%, and patches of bare skin start to appear
  • Sessions 6 to 8: around 70 to 90% across most areas

Aftercare:

  • Shave between sessions, but don't wax or thread, since the laser needs the root in place
  • Keep the treated area out of direct sun for a few days
  • Use SPF 30 or higher regularly

FAQs

What makes Soprano ICE different from other lasers?

It combines three wavelengths, keeps the handpiece moving and cools the skin the whole time. That makes it more comfortable and better suited to darker skin than many older lasers.

Does Soprano ICE hurt?

Most people feel a warm tingle, often compared to a warm-stone massage. The cooling keeps things comfortable.

How many sessions do I need?

Usually 6 to 8, four to six weeks apart. The upper lip and other hormonal areas can need a few more.

Is it safe for dark Indian skin?

Yes. The 1064nm wavelength and constant cooling make it suitable for darker skin, with settings chosen by your specialist.

Can it shape my beard or eyebrows?

Yes. A small facial tip is used for beard lines and brows, and also for ear and nostril hair.

Can I shave between sessions?

Yes, shaving is fine. Avoid waxing and threading until your course is finished.

How long does a session take?

A few minutes for small areas like the upper lip, and around 45 to 60 minutes for full legs or the back.

What does Soprano ICE cost in Delhi?

Around ₹1,500 per session for small areas, up to about ₹40,000 for a full body session. The final cost is confirmed after your consultation.
