Before understanding the treatments available, it helps to understand what actually causes pores to appear enlarged in the first place. Pores are tiny openings in the skin through which the sebaceous glands release sebum the skin's natural oil. They are a necessary and normal part of healthy skin. The problem arises when the pores become visibly enlarged and remain open consistently. This happens due to several causes that are very common among people in Delhi and across India. The most frequent cause is excess oil production from overactive sebaceous glands which can be triggered by hormonal changes during puberty, the menstrual cycle, pregnancy, or conditions like PCOD, as well as by stress and certain medications like oral contraceptive pills. When these glands produce more oil than the skin needs, it collects inside the pore, stretching it open. When the oil in an open pore comes into contact with air and oxidises, it turns dark this is how blackheads form. When the pore is covered by skin cells and oil becomes trapped beneath, whiteheads develop instead. Another major cause is the natural loss of collagen and elastin that happens with age. As these structural proteins reduce, the skin loses its elasticity and the tissue around the pores becomes looser, making the pores appear larger and more stretched even if the actual oil production has not changed. Thick hair follicles and genetic predisposition are also contributing factors that some people simply inherit and cannot change. Sun damage from UV exposure without adequate SPF further weakens the collagen around pores and accelerates their visible enlargement over time. In Delhi's environment specifically, the combination of pollution, UV rays, heat, and humidity makes all of these factors worse which is exactly why open pores are such a widespread concern in the city.
