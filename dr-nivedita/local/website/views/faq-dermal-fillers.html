1. What are dermal fillers?

Dermal fillers are substances that are injected beneath the skin’s surface to smooth out wrinkles and add volume and contour to your face. The products are composed of the stabilised hyaluronic acid (HA) which occurs naturally in the skin cells. It works by drawing in water and therefore alleviating the skin, thus reducing the treated wrinkles. It is not permanent by any means and is totally biodegradable. Depending on the type of dermal fillers, results can last up to 1-2 years as per the best cosmetic dermatologists in Delhi.

For instance, the lips are injected to improve their shape, size, symmetry, and proportion. The cheeks are injected to make the face more defined and attractive. An underdeveloped chin is injected to make it appear more proportionate. The nose is injected to improve the shape and its contour.

6. Are there any side effects?

Filler injections may have some minor or temporary side effects where they were injected. If performed by a qualified aesthetic doctor in Delhi, one does not need to worry about any severe or long term side effects.
Usually people experience:

  • Redness
  • Swelling
  • Tenderness
  • Bruising (occasionally)

The side effects tend to disappear within 7-14 days, if not earlier.

Serious side effects are more likely to occur when the person injecting the filler does not have adequate medical training and experience. To be safe and healthy, you should never get filler injections in a non-medical and illegal setting, such as a non-medical spa, salon, or someone’s home advises Dr. Dadu, the best dermatologist in Delhi.

When fillers are injected in a non-medical or unauthorized setting or by an inexperienced provider, serious side effects increase dramatically. These side effects include:

  • Lumps.
  • Ridges.
  • Over-filled areas.
  • Allergic reaction.
  • Skin discoloration.
  • Infection.
  • Severe swelling.

9. Will it look natural?

To get natural-looking results after the injection, the person injecting the filler must have expertise in placing the filler.

The best aesthetic dermatologists in Delhi recommend not overdoing the fillers. Your results will look more natural with a minimalist approach. For example, patients who have very thin lips should not get dramatically fuller or thick lips. Creating fuller lips on those patients could be extremely unflattering because the lips might start to resemble a duck’s bill.
