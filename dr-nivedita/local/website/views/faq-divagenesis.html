FAQs

1. What is Divagenesis?

DivaGenesis is a blend of the latest technologies which not only helps in achieving an instant party glow but also hydrates the skin by making it healthier and supple. It is one of the advanced methods for skin lightening which is exclusively tailor-made and customized at Dr. Nivedita Dadu's Dermatology Clinic.

2. What results can be expected from the treatment?

After treatment, one can expect rejuvenated and glowing skin. The skin will feel smoother and even toned after the session. One can experience a gradual reduction in pigmentation, fine lines, age spots and photo-damage.

3. Can I apply makeup after treatment?

Yes, you can apply makeup 6 to 12 hours after the treatment.

4. What is the downtime after treatment?

There is no downtime after the treatment. The treatment leaves the skin hydrated and rejuvenated and one can go back to their normal routine straight after the treatment.

5. Is there any side effect?

There are no known side effects of the treatment. It is completely safe for all skin types and all age groups.

6. How long does the treatment last?

It will take around an hour to perform the treatment.

7. Are the results permanent?

Results after one session will last for a certain time period. It is advisable to go for successive sessions for long-lasting results.

8. When can I repeat the session?

The session can be repeated at a gap of 3 to 4 weeks.

9. How is it different from a traditional laser?

DivaGenesis is a combination of advanced lasers along with micro-channeling technology. The laser penetrates the dermal layers and targets the pigment that causes dark spots. The procedure is followed by an innovative Skin Rejuvenation System that works by micro-channeling and suction component to gently lift the skin. The fine needles create micro-channels for the infusion of serum cocktails without any redness or downtime.

10. Who is the right candidate for this treatment?

Anyone who is above 18 years of age can opt for this treatment. This is a skin treatment for those who are looking for lunchtime option for skin rejuvenation and instant party glow which require no downtime.
