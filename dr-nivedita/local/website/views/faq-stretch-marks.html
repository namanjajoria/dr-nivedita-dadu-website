1. WHAT ARE THE CAUSES OF STRETCH MARKS?

The causes of stretch marks vary from individualto individual. Generally the resons for stretch marks are excessive weight gain or excessive weight loss and pregnancy. In short, when your skin stretches itself the stretch marks are emerged.

2. MY STRETCH MARKS WERE INITIALLY RED BUT NOW THEY ARE WHITE. WHAT DOES THAT MEAN?

Stretch marks vary in color and appearance. They can be classified under two types

  • Red stretch marks (striae rubra) form because of the stretching of the dermal layer of the skin, permitting the blood vessels in the layer beneath to show through.
  • White stretch marks, also known as striae alba appear on the places where the blood vessels have receded, showing fat below the dermal layer. This explains their silvery white appearance.

3. HOW TO AVOID GETTING STRETCH MARKS IN THE FIRST PLACE?

Avoiding stretch marks is a matter of staying hydrated and keeping your skin moisturized. Here are few tips to help you keep your skin stretch mark-free:

  • Drink ample Amount of Pure and Clean Water. Staying hydrated actually has a lot of positive effects on your body. It helps in the prevention of stretch marks as it provides the skin sufficient moisture to stop these marks from showing up when you put on weight or build muscles.
  • Apply Coconut Oil to Stretchmark-Prone Areas. Coconut oil can also function as a moisturizer to help your skin stay moisturized, exfoliate theouter layer and give your skin extra elasticity. This will prevent the breaking of the dermal layer and the development of stretch marks.
  • Eat healthy. Nourishing your body with wholesome foods lets you maintain a healthy balance of vitamins and minerals. You can also avoid the fluctuations in your weight that can cause stretch marks by eating a balanced diet . It helps you maintain a constant weight and prevents you from gaining fat, which usually causes stretch marks to form on your abdomen and thighs.
  • Avoid Alcohol. As much as possible, avoid excessive intake of alcohol. Alcohol is known to suck out the moisture from your body, which will make your skin more drier than normal. This dryness will then increase the risk of stretch mark formation.
  • Stock Up on Vitamin E. Vitamin E is famous for its role in maintaining skin health and preserving the collagen found in the skin, which helps the skin to maintain its elasticity.
  • Exercise Regularly. Regular exercising will keep your skin taut and your weight in check, avoiding sudden weight gain or loss.
