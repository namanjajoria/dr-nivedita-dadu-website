Get your hair fall cured with Mesotherapy

Hair fall is most commonly prevalent in both men & women. It is a condition where the hair follicles start to lose the strength of the hair and start to fall in a systematic way. Hair is considered to be in the constant cycle of shedding, growth, and renewal. When it comes to specific details of hair loss in men and women, there are differences. From the causes to the nature of the problem, there are several remarkable differences that one can spot.

Causes of Hair Fall
Seasonal hair fall is a very common thing but your hair fall may also depend on your gender, your age, your pattern of hair thinning, your lifestyle and other diseases like thyroid or PCOS. There are numerous reasons due to which people face excessive hair fall.

  • Any kind of physical or emotional stress
  • Consumption of alcohol and smoking
  • Due to pregnancy & weight loss
  • Certain medication that might cause excessive hair fall
  • Having a long-standing illness or surgery or cosmetic procedures like straightening, smoothening and colouring.
