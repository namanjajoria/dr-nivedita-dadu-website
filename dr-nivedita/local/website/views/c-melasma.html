Melasma is a tan or dark skin discoloration. Melasma is particularly common in women, especially pregnant women and those who are taking oral or patch contraceptives or hormone replacement therapy (HRT) medications. The symptoms of Melasma are dark, uneven well demarcated hyper pigmented patches commonly found on the upper cheek, nose, lips, upper lip, and forehead.
