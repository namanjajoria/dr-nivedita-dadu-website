1. What is DMC-Expressolift®?

DMC-Expressolift® is a non-surgical, non-invasive skin tightening and skin rejuvenating treatment.

This treatment uses infrared light energy which penetrates the tissue under the skin’s surface without causing any damage to the top surface layer. It is considered to be one of the best skin tightening treatments in Delhi.

2. How does it work?

In the DMC-Expressolift® procedure, we at Dr. Nivedita Dadu’s Dermatology Clinic use a safe level of infrared light that can penetrate the deeper layers of the skin. The light energy heats the water in the skin tissue which leads the collagen fibers to contract and tighten. It also stimulates fibroblasts which repair the skin cells and produce new collagen. This helps to smoothen fine lines and wrinkles, giving a long-lasting firmness to saggy skin explains Dr. Nivedita Dadu, the best dermatologist in Delhi. One can get rejuvenating results because the DMC-Expressolift® light energy only heats the deeper layer of skin that contains collagen, it leaves the top surface layer unaffected. The top layer will get repaired when deeper layers get their protein. Hence, it is one of the safest and most effective skin tightening treatments in Delhi.

3. What happens during the treatment?

Initially, we apply a cooling gel to your skin. After the application of cooling gel, we slew the DMC-Expressolift® handpiece over the targeted areas of your skin which heats your skin until it reaches a temperature between 39 and 42 degrees Centigrade. We work at this temperature for a further 10 to 15 minutes. The procedure doesn’t consume much downtime it gets finished in about 30 to 40 minutes. To know the cost of skin tightening treatment in Delhi, visit Dr. Nivedita Dadu’s Dermatology Clinic.

4. Is the treatment painful?

No, the treatment is not painful. One does not feel any pain during NIR [near-infrared] skin tightening treatment. There will be a warm sensation which can be slightly uncomfortable for some people. However, most people find the procedure very comfortable. The handpiece comes with a built-in cooling system that is very helpful in preventing too much heat on the skin’s surface layer. If someone starts feeling too warm, more cooling gel can be applied during the treatment explains Dr. Nivedita Dadu, the best skin specialist in Delhi.

5. Are there any side effects?

Yes, it has a couple of side effects. Since the treatment involves heat to your skin at a relatively high temperature, you should expect some redness afterward. You may also experience some mild swelling, but this and the redness should settle down within a few hours (24 hours at the most). There are no other after-effects and you can continue with your day immediately after treatment. For more details about this treatment visit Dr. Nivedita Dadu’s Dermatology clinic, the best skin clinic in Delhi.

6. How soon will I see the results?

The skin may start looking firmer immediately after the treatment and may observe some swelling. This swelling generally reduces within 24 hours after which the treated skin feels smoother and tighter. However, to see the complete effect it may take from 2 or 3 months, says the best dermatologist in Delhi. As the skin starts building new collagen during this period, tightening of the skin becomes more visible.

7. How many treatments will I need?

It depends on your skin. Some patients have achieved their expected results after the first treatment. Most people benefit from a course of 3 to 6 treatments, at intervals of up to 3 weeks. A few patients sometimes need up to 6-8 treatments, depending on their skin condition and expectations. During your consultation for the procedure, we shall discuss the number and frequency of treatments that you would require to achieve optimal results.

8. How long do the results last?

DMC-Expressolift® treatment helps to reduce some of the visible signs of skin ageing. Usually, the results from the treatment last for a year at the least. DMC-Expressolift® can’t, however, completely halt the ageing process, and you may continue to need top-up treatments to maintain results.
