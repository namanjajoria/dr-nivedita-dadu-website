What causes hair loss in women?

In women, there are several other reasons than men that may cause hair loss.

Hormonal imbalances:Altered menstrual cycle is one of the indications of hormonal imbalances in female (polycystic ovaries). Besides this, dearranged thyroid profile is another common hormonal cause for hair fall.
Pregnancy:The hormonal changes that take place during pregnancy might cause hair loss in women.
Diet:Lack of protein in women can lead to hair loss. Besides this vitamin D, calcium, iron or vitamin B12 deficiency can also cause hair fall.
Stress:Work pressure, family issues or any other kind of emotional trauma can cause hair loss in female.
Outdoor Effect: Excessive sun exposure, pollution, dirt and dust have also their respective roles to play when it comes to hair loss in women.
Genetic causes: It is noted that genes have also a part in hair loss. Women who have family members with baldness may get it.
Diseases: Infections like typhoid, dengue. etc can precipitate acute hair loss in female.
Lifestyle: Chemically treated hair or excessive use of hair equipment may damage the hair cuticle and make it more prone to breakage.
Medication: oral medicines( some antihypertensives, antidepressants etc.) taken for a long time may cause hair fall.

Prevention of hair loss in women:

While some factors are really out of your hand (though fixed easily by treatment) you can try following some tips to prevent hair loss in female.

Proper diet: Balance your diet properly. Ensure that you have proper intakes of vitamins and proteins in your food. Pregnant women need to pay more attention to their diet.
Avoid over Use of products and overindulging in hair treatments like smoothening, straightening & colouring. Choose your hair product wisely and use them in moderation.
Hair protection: Protect your hair from dirt, dust, and pollution.
