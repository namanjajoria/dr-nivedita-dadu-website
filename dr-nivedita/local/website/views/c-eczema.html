Causes: As we already said, the root cause of eczema is unknown but it is a kind of allergy. There are definite triggers which aggravate the allergy.
1. Harsh chemicals – may be in the form of soap, detergents, shampoo or any other cosmetic products.
2. As one of the clear symptoms of Eczema is dry and itchy skin rashes, it is advisable to keep your skin moist. However, low humidity and high humidity both should be avoided as one may cause the excessive dryness, while the other causes itching due to sweating.
3. People with Asthma are more prone to Eczema.
4. Other allergies such as food allergies, allergy from pet hair and dust mites also worsen the effects of eczema.
5. Stress is another major trigger for Eczema and it may compel the need for itching.
